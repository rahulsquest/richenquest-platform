## 1 · Brand voice
Trustworthy · research-driven · premium · global · educational — expressed as:

**Specific over general.** Not "Europe is affordable" — "Debrecen is ₹30 lakh, Pécs is ₹22 lakh,
and here's the fee page for both."
**Sourced over asserted.** Every number carries where it came from.
**Willing to say "we don't know."** *"We've verified 2 of 21. Here's why we won't guess about
the rest."*
**Never hype.** No "🚀", no "life-changing", no guarantees.

## 3 · The post format — every post, no exceptions

```
HOOK      one line, specific and surprising, no throat-clearing
PROBLEM   what the reader currently believes or is about to do
INSIGHT   the verified finding + WHERE IT CAME FROM
CTA       one action, matched to intent
```

**Worked example:**
> **HOOK** A student applied to an Italian university three weeks before the deadline. She was on time. She still lost €14,000.
> **PROBLEM** Everyone tracks the university's application deadline. Italy has a second one.
> **INSIGHT** The regional DSU scholarship bando closes *before* the university's deadline — a separate body, a separate date, and if you miss it the grant is gone for the year. Emilia-Romagna's own bando says the documents must be "complete, translated and legalized" by the deadline, "under penalty of being ineligible." [link]
> **CTA** If you're looking at Italy for 2027, check your *regional* deadline, not just the university's.

## 4 · Cadence — honest about what is survivable

The brief proposes **13 pieces/week** (5 LinkedIn, 5 Instagram, 3 Shorts) from a founder who
is also counselling, researching universities and chasing partnerships.

**That is not survivable solo, and the failure mode is worse than a lower cadence**: two good
weeks, then silence, then a restart that reads as abandonment. Consistency compounds;
intensity does not.

| | Proposed | **Recommended** | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 5/wk | **3/wk** | Highest-value audience: parents, partners, universities |
| Instagram | 5/wk | **2/wk** + repurpose | Student reach; carousels from LinkedIn posts |
| YouTube Shorts | 3/wk | **1/wk** | Highest production cost per unit |
| **Total** | 13 | **6** | Sustainable for 90 days without a team |

**One research finding → one LinkedIn post → one carousel → one Short.** One piece of work,
four outputs. Scale cadence when someone else is producing, not before.

## 5 · Founder brand — Rahul

**Position:** *the founder who checks the numbers himself.* Not a guru, not a motivator — the
person who found that a university's own page was two years stale and published it.

**Weekly:** 3 LinkedIn posts (1 pillar-2 deep, 1 pillar-3 mistake, 1 pillar-4 journey) ·
1 short video · answer every comment personally for the first 90 days.

**Never publish:** invented student counts · testimonials before students exist · "we help
1000+ students" · any guarantee · anything unlinkable. The success-stories section of the
website is deliberately empty — **that is the brand, and it must stay consistent across
channels.**

**No email gate on any of them.** Gating a free report to harvest an address contradicts a
brand built on not hiding things. The CTA is the portal, and the portal is where consent is
properly taken.

## 7 · Workflow — research → publish

```
MONDAY     research block (2h) → one verified finding, with its link
TUESDAY    write: LinkedIn long-form + carousel outline
WEDNESDAY  publish LinkedIn #1 · record the Short
THURSDAY   publish Instagram carousel + Short
FRIDAY     publish LinkedIn #2 (mistake) · answer every comment
SATURDAY   publish LinkedIn #3 (journey) · log what performed
```

**Tools, deliberately minimal:** Canva (design) · phone camera (video) · native scheduling ·
native analytics. **No paid stack until a channel is proven.** Buying tooling before an
audience is how solo founders spend money on the feeling of progress.
