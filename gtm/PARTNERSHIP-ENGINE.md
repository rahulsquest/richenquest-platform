## Four target types, and they want different things

| Target | What they actually want | What RichenQuest offers | Realistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Universities** (private) | enrolled students | qualified, document-complete applicants | commission — the standard model |
| **Public universities** | good applicants; **they pay agents little or nothing** | prepared students | little/no commission — **so the family pays** |
| **Regional scholarship bodies** (ER.GO, EDISU, ERDIS, ERSU) | **fewer incomplete applications** | students whose legalised documents arrive complete and on time | **credibility and access, not commission** |
| **Mentors** | meaningful contribution, some income | matched students, verified profile | commission on sessions |

**The scholarship bodies are the most interesting and the least obvious.** They are not
customers — they are the gatekeepers of €14–16k per student, and an incomplete file is pure
administrative waste to them. A consultancy that reliably delivers complete files is solving
*their* problem. That relationship cannot be bought and compounds.

## Why a partner would work with RichenQuest — the honest pitch

Not "we send volume." Anyone can say that, and most agents send noise.

> **"We won't send you a student whose documents aren't complete. We verify tuition, living
> cost and deadlines against your own published pages before we ever quote them to a family —
> and when your page is out of date, we'll tell you."**

That last clause is real and demonstrable: Debrecen's February-intake page showed a **2024
deadline for a 2025 intake**. Telling an institution their own page is stale is a genuine
favour and an unusually good opening.

## Pipeline process

| Stage | Action | Gate to advance |
|---|---|---|
| **Identified** | in CRM, `Partnership_Stage = Identified` | serves the ₹10–25L segment |
| **Contacted** | verification email — facts first, never a partnership pitch | a reply |
| **In Discussion** | call; ask what makes *their* job harder | mutual interest |
| **Agreement Drafted** | terms; **commission never assumed** | founder + advocate |
| **Signed** | `Agreement_Signed_On`, `Agreement_Expires_On` | — |
| **Active** | students actually flowing | ≥1 applicant |

**Contact with a factual question, not a pitch.** All six emails asked only for missing data.
That is a lower-friction opening than a partnership request and it produces a usable answer
either way.

## Rules
Never claim a partnership that is not signed · never imply commission where none is agreed ·
never present a university as a "partner" on any student-facing surface without a signed
agreement · `Commission_Status` stays `Unknown` until it is confirmed **in writing**.
