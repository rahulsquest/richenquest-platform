## Rules
**Never publish a claim you cannot link.** One invented statistic destroys more than a year of
posting builds — and this brand is *specifically* built on checkability.

**Post 20 is the highest-risk and highest-value item.** Publishing "we have placed nobody" is
uncomfortable and it is the most credible thing on the list. Do not soften it.

**Repurpose, don't recreate:** one research finding → LinkedIn long-form → Instagram carousel
→ Short. Weeks 2 and 3 are already written above from existing repo material.

**Measure:** conversations started, not likes. A post that produces one teacher conversation
beat a post with 400 impressions and none.
