## The unit that matters
A ₹10–25L family in Bihar or Nepal. **Debrecen at ₹30 lakh is outside that band** — real,
verified, and the wrong default. **Italy with DSU at ₹0–3.5 lakh is inside it**, and its
difficulty is why the family cannot self-serve. The business is priced against difficulty,
not against a university list.

## Stream 2 — SaaS subscription *(not yet, and here is the honest reason)*

Proposed at ₹999–1,999/month. **A subscription must deliver recurring value, and with 2
verified opportunities the matching output does not change month to month.** Charging monthly
for a static answer is churn with extra steps.

**Precondition: ~20 verified opportunities + live deadline intelligence.** Then the recurring
value is real — deadlines move, bandi open, requirements change, and the platform tells you.

**The recurring services that already justify money** (from the ledger, and genuinely annual):
residence-permit renewal · **DSU credit compliance** (protects €14–16k every year) ·
insurance renewal. **That is the honest subscription** — it is service-backed, not access-backed.

## Stream 3 — Mentor marketplace *(blocked, correctly)*
Commission on paid sessions. **0 verified mentors.** The model is ready and the inventory is
empty, and inventing a mentor is the single most damaging fabrication available. Not a
revenue line until real people with checkable credentials exist.

## Stream 4 — University partnerships
Commission or service fee for qualified, document-complete applicants. **Status: 1 pending,
0 signed.** Note the structural fact: Italian public universities pay agents almost nothing,
which is precisely why the Italy product is **family-paid**. Do not model Italy revenue as
commission.

## Stream 5 — B2B intelligence
Country pathway data, deadline intelligence, market insight for institutions and regional
bodies. **Real, and last.** It becomes credible only once the opportunity graph is large and
demonstrably accurate — selling intelligence you have not yet used yourself is not a product.
