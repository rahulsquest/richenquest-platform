## The ratio that tells you what is broken

```
conversations → qualified → calls → conversions
```

| Where it breaks | What it means | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| Conversations low | outreach didn't happen | **nothing else matters — do the block** |
| Conversations high, qualified low | wrong audience or wrong offer | who you're talking to |
| Qualified high, calls low | booking friction | the ask |
| Calls high, conversions low | the offer, the price, or the 2-opportunity portfolio | **check the portfolio before the pitch** |

**Diagnose top-down.** A conversion problem is almost never a conversion problem when the top
of the funnel is empty.

## Also logged weekly — three lines, free text
- **Best conversation this week** and why
- **Most common objection**, verbatim
- **What I said that didn't land**

Objections are the product roadmap. They are worth more than the six numbers, and they are
the only thing here that cannot be reconstructed later.

## Reviewed monthly, not weekly
Verified opportunities · pilot corrections · university replies · content reach.
Weekly noise on slow-moving numbers causes overreaction.

## The stop-and-reassess line
**If 30 conversations produce fewer than 5 qualified leads, the problem is the offer or the
audience — not the funnel, not the content, and definitely not the software.** Stop and find
out which before adding anything.

## What is deliberately not tracked
Followers · impressions · likes · website traffic. They move without students moving, and a
number that can rise while the business stands still is a distraction with a chart.
