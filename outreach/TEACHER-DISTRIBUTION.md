# Teacher-seeded distribution — the one system

**The chosen engine. Not seminars, not ads, not social media.** One message, to teachers, who are
already inside the parent WhatsApp groups RichenQuest can never join.

## Why this and nothing else

| | |
|---|---|
| **It is a message, not an event** | A seminar needs a date, a room, travel and a calendar. This needs a send button |
| **Teachers are already inside parent groups** | A teacher sits in 2–4 groups of 80–150 parents. **We cannot join those groups. They are already in them** |
| **Forwarding raises the teacher's standing** | People forward what makes them look useful to people they care about. **A free, honest cost report does exactly that** |
| **Parents hold the money** | Students forward to peers. Teachers forward to parents |
| **It works while nobody is available** | No booking, no travel, no dependency on anyone answering the phone |

**Reach arithmetic:** 20 teachers contacted → ~8 forward → ~250 parents each → **~2,000 parents seen**.
At a 1–2% enquiry rate that is **20–40 enquiries, ~10–20 qualified**. Repeat with 100 teachers over
30 days to approach 100.

## The rules that keep this from becoming spam

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Individually, never a broadcast list** | A visible group send reads as spam and burns the contact permanently |
| **"If it isn't useful, ignore this — I won't follow up"** | And then actually don't. **One follow-up maximum, after 10 days** |
| **Nothing to buy in the report** | The moment it contains a sales pitch, teachers stop forwarding |
| **No teacher incentive, ever** | A paid forward is an advertisement. **An unpaid one is a recommendation, and only one of them works** |
| **The report stays free** | Constitution, principle 6 |

## Tracking — without which this is just activity

Every enquiry gets `Lead_Source_Detail` = **the teacher's name**. That single field answers the only
question that matters: **which teachers actually forward.** Ten who do are worth a hundred who agree
to.

**Target: 20 teachers in week 1. Measure forwards, not sends.**

## What comes second, not first

**Google Business Profile.** Free, highest-intent, and compounds — but it needs identity verification
and 2–4 weeks before it ranks, and it needs reviews we do not have. **Start the verification now so it
is live when the first students arrive; do not wait on it for the first leads.**

## Deleted for the first 100 students

**SEO** (6–12 months) · **Instagram, YouTube, Threads** (months to build an audience) · **Reddit and
Quora** (wrong geography) · **Telegram** (no existing communities) · **paid ads** (no fee data, no
conversion rate) · **Medium and LinkedIn articles** (not where Bihar parents are).

**Each is defensible later. None produces a lead before November.**
