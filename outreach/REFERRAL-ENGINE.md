# REFERRAL ENGINE — the cheapest students RichenQuest will ever get

> **The largest untapped asset in this business is a past-student base that has never been asked for
> anything.** `claims.json` records **1,000+ students guided**. The CRM holds **zero leads**.
>
> **F6 has been open for many phases** — not because it is hard, but because 50 individually written
> messages is two hours nobody starts. These are written. Personalise the name and send.

## Rules that make this work rather than backfire

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Send individually. Never a broadcast list.** | A visible group message reads as spam and burns the contact permanently |
| **Ask for an introduction, not a sale** | A past student turned into a salesperson stops replying. An introduction costs them nothing |
| **No referral fee named yet** | A referral fee is a fraction of a price that does not exist. **Wait for F1** — naming a number now means changing it later |
| **Say what is not known** | These messages claim no scholarship, no guarantee, no visa odds. That is the brand |
| **Never automated** | Referral messages from a person convert. From a system, they are spam |

## Where to start, if you only do one

**B — coaching institutes and school teachers.** They meet the target demographic in cohorts, at
exactly the age of the decision, and in Bihar and Jharkhand they are trusted more than any brand.
**Ten conversations there is worth a hundred cold messages.**

## Tracking

Every referred enquiry must be created as a Lead with **`Lead_Source_Detail` = the referrer's name**.
That single field turns this from goodwill into a measurable channel, and it is what tells you which
of the four audiences above is actually worth repeating next cycle.

`qualifyLead` will then score readiness and flag duplicates automatically, and `leadToPlan` turns any
of them into a counselling-ready plan in one call.

## The one number to watch

**Enquiries per 10 messages sent.** Below 1, the message is wrong. Above 3, stop reading this file
and send more messages.
