# EMAIL-IDENTITY-DECISION.md — FOUNDER DECISION REQUIRED

**Not decided automatically.** This has legal and business implications: the address named in
a privacy notice is where DPDP rights requests must be received, and changing it later means
re-issuing documents already given to families.

## What exists today

| Address | Status | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| `official@richenquest.com` | **The only connected mailbox** — 4 verification emails sent from it 2026-08-23 | Mail.app; universities now have it |
| `support@richenquest.com` | Supplied as the production support address | **Privacy policy, T&C, refund policy, grievance officer contact, all client templates** |
| `info@richenquest.com` | Supplied as alternate | Legal pack placeholder table |

## The problem, concretely
Four universities have been asked to reply to `official@`. Every legal document tells students
and regulators to write to `support@`. If a family exercises a DPDP erasure right at
`support@` and nobody reads that mailbox, the company misses a statutory deadline.

## Options

**A. Make `support@` primary.** Matches every legal document already drafted. Requires the
mailbox to actually exist and be monitored, and `official@` to forward to it — four
universities will reply there.

**B. Make `official@` primary.** Matches the working mailbox. Requires editing the privacy
policy, T&C, refund policy, grievance contact and all client templates.

**C. Keep both, forward one to the other.** Lowest effort; risks two inboxes, one unread.

## Recommendation — **Option A**, with a condition
`support@` is already the address of record in documents that carry legal weight, and
rewriting a grievance contact after publication is worse than configuring a forward. Set
`official@` to forward to `support@` so the four pending university replies are not lost.

**Condition:** the grievance officer named in the privacy policy (Rahul Kumar) must actually
read whichever mailbox is chosen. DPDP requires a reachable human, not an address.

## Decide before
The first real student submits, because that is the moment the privacy notice becomes a
binding promise.
