# OPPORTUNITY-GRAPH-STRATEGY.md — 2026-08-23

## 0 · The finding that reframes everything

**The two opportunities that are rankable today serve the wrong demographic.**

Debrecen is a **₹30 lakh** first year. RichenQuest's stated segment is **₹10–25 lakh**. The
repo already names this: *"We are spending a 7-week February window on a ₹30 lakh product that
does not serve our demographic."*

So "20 rankable opportunities" is the wrong target if the 20 are priced like the 2. The right
target is **20 opportunities a ₹10–25L family can actually use**, and that changes the
country mix, the intake, and the order of work.

## 4 · Italy strategy
See `ITALY-OPPORTUNITY-STRATEGY.md`. Three points that belong here:

- **Italy with DSU is ₹0–3.5 lakh** against Debrecen's ₹30 lakh. It is the only option in
  reach of the actual segment.
- **It is a September 2027 market.** Adding Italian universities does **nothing** for a
  February 2027 pilot — recorded ☠️ DEAD in the ledger.
- **Rank by regional accommodation capacity, not DSU generosity** — the grant is nationally
  floored, so generosity barely varies. This corrects the brief's premise.
