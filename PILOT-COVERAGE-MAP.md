## What moved this session

**Living-cost coverage 4 → 14.** Germany's requirement is a **national** figure, not a
per-university one: DAAD publishes an average spend of **876 EUR/month** and a visa
proof-of-funds requirement of **992 EUR/month = 11,904/year**. One authoritative source
served all 10 German records. IU International independently corroborates the same figure.

The higher number was recorded, consistent with the asymmetric rule used throughout: the
**lower** bound of a student's budget, the **upper** bound of a cost.

## The structural finding — 8 universities, 3 countries, one pattern

| Publishes | Reliably |
|---|---|
| Tuition | **Public** universities: yes. **Private** universities: calculator- or portal-gated |
| Living cost | Often, and sometimes nationally (DAAD, Hungarian university pages) |
| **Future-intake deadline** | **Almost never** |

Debrecen, Pécs and Szeged — all Hungarian **public** — published usable data. METU, Vistula,
EU Business School and IU — all **private** — did not. IU's tuition sits behind a fee
calculator; its deadlines are named only as "April and October" intakes.

**Half the portfolio (10 of 21) is German private universities** — the hardest tier to
verify, the most expensive, and the least aligned with the ₹10–25L segment the business
actually serves.

## Recommendation

1. **Do not chase the German private block.** Ten records, four gate fields each, all
   calculator-gated. Low yield per hour and poor segment fit.
2. **Add Hungarian, Polish and Czech public universities instead.** Public institutions in
   this region publish exactly the fields the gate requires. That is where 20 rankable
   opportunities is actually reachable.
3. **Capture the Italy knowledge that already exists in the business** before researching
   Italy from scratch.
4. **Germany needs one more field the CRM does not have:** Indian students require an **APS
   certificate**, which takes months. Any German recommendation without that caveat is
   misleading about the timeline. Not added — schema changes were out of scope this session.
