How independence is protected

The structure behind the advice

We work with universities, employers and institutions, and intend to work with more. What follows is the structure that keeps those relationships out of the advice you receive.

Independence
A recommendation is independent when it can be reproduced from documented criteria that reference no commercial term.
Compensation firewall
No one’s pay, bonus or review here varies according to which institution, country or programme you choose.
Identical terms
Any payment from an institution is at a published rate identical across all institutions. No volume tiers, no preferential placement.
Steering requests
Requests for preferential placement are declined and recorded. We decline the request, not the relationship.

What we will not say

Claims we do not make

These are enforced by our build: the site will not publish if any of them appear. They are checked on every change, not on request.

  • No visa or admission success percentages — we have no dataset that would make one honest.
  • No university described as our partner until an agreement is signed.
  • No student count beyond the figures in the register above.
  • No testimonial or photograph without written consent from the person in it.
  • No guarantee of any outcome we do not control.
  • No manufactured urgency — no countdowns, no “limited seats”.

If you find something on this site that breaches the above, tell us and we will correct it publicly, with the date, rather than quietly editing it away.
