Questions families ask first

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to study in Europe?

Often far less than families expect. Public universities in Germany and Norway charge little or no tuition, and in Italy the DSU grant can bring costs close to zero for eligible families. Exact numbers depend on country and program — we walk you through real figures in your free consultation.

Can I really get a fully-funded scholarship?

Some funding is competitive (DAAD, MEXT, Erasmus Mundus); some is need-based and awarded by right if you qualify (Italy's DSU). We assess your profile honestly and only pursue what you can genuinely win.

Can I work part-time while studying?

Most European destinations allow international students to work around 20 hours a week during term — enough to help with living costs. Rules vary by country and visa type; your counselor confirms the exact limits for your destination.

Do I need IELTS to apply?

Not always. Many universities accept alternatives — other English tests or Medium-of-Instruction letters — though some countries and visas do require a formal test. We advise on your specific case before you spend on exams.
