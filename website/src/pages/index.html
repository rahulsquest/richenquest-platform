Why Europe, why now

The map has changed

The four countries everyone applied to are tightening. Europe is opening its doors — and we are already there.

The old routes are narrowing

Canada, the USA, the UK and Australia tightened student visas, caps and costs through 2024–26. Families need a smarter map — not more hope.

Europe is opening up

Germany, France, Ireland and Italy are actively growing international intakes — with English-taught programs and public universities that charge little or no tuition.

We are already on the ground

Counselors in India and in Italy. That means European office hours, local knowledge of how these systems actually work, and someone who can answer at the hour the university replies — not twelve hours later.
