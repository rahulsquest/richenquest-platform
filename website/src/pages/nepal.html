The Nepal-specific step

Your NOC, handled without drama

Every Nepali student needs a No Objection Certificate from the Ministry of Education before paying fees abroad or applying for a visa. It's straightforward — when done in the right order.

1

Offer letter first

Secure admission from your university abroad — the NOC application is tied to a specific institution and program.

2

Apply for the NOC

Apply to the Ministry's NOC section (online or in person) with your documents. We prepare the file so it goes through the first time.

3

Pay fees & file your visa

With the NOC issued, tuition transfers and your visa application proceed legally and smoothly — in the right sequence, with nothing to redo.
