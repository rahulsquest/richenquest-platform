Why Japan

What you actually get

The MEXT scholarship

Japan's government scholarship covers tuition, flights and a monthly stipend — genuinely fully funded, genuinely competitive. We tell you honestly whether your profile can compete.

Predictable public costs

National university tuition is standardised (≈ ¥535,800/year) — no fee lottery, and quality is uniformly high.

Work-friendly rules

Students may work up to 28 hours a week — the most generous allowance among our destinations — and Japan actively needs young workers.

Japan FAQs

What families ask us about Japan

Do I need Japanese?

English-taught degrees exist and are growing, but Japanese transforms your options — many students take a language-school year first. We'll tell you honestly which route fits your goals and budget.

Why is Japan so popular from Nepal?

Established communities, work-friendly student rules and clear language-school pathways have made Japan the #1 choice for Nepali students — we know this corridor well.

How competitive is MEXT?

Very. It's a genuine full ride, so thousands apply. Strong academics and a clear study plan matter; we assess your chances honestly before you invest months in it.
