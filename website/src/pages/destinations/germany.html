APS: the step that decides your timeline

Students applying from India need an APS certificate — an academic verification by the APS office — before a German student visa application. Processing takes weeks and cannot be rushed, which makes APS the single most common reason students miss their intake.

Our rule: APS starts on day one of your Germany plan, in parallel with university applications — never after an admission letter arrives. We track it as a hard deadline in your case file.

Germany FAQs

What families ask us about Germany

Is Germany really tuition-free?

At public universities in most federal states, yes — you pay only a semester contribution of roughly €150–350. Baden-Württemberg charges tuition to non-EU students, and private institutions charge their own fees. Living costs are the real budget item.

How much money must I show in the blocked account?

Currently around €11,900 for a year, deposited before your visa application; German authorities update the figure periodically, and we confirm the current amount when you apply. Every Germany-bound student needs this plus health insurance.

Can I study in English in Germany?

Widely at master's level, and increasingly for bachelor's. For daily life and part-time jobs, basic German helps a lot — start learning before you fly.

What are my chances of staying after graduation?

Germany offers an 18-month job-seeker visa after graduation, and engineering, IT and healthcare graduates are actively recruited. No one can promise you a job — we can promise you a realistic plan for finding one.
