Why France

What you actually get

Subsidised public tuition

Non-EU students pay a differentiated public rate of roughly €3,000–4,000 a year — and many institutions and programs grant partial or full exemptions.

National momentum

France is actively recruiting Indian students, with government programs and growing English-taught options in business, science and the arts.

Career doors

Internship culture (stage), global companies, and a job-search stay after a master's make France more practical than its romantic image suggests.

France FAQs

What families ask us about France

Do I need French?

Not for English-taught programs, which are growing fast — but everyday life rewards basic French, and it widens your program choices considerably.

What is Campus France?

The official process students from India and Nepal go through — profile, interview and visa routing. We prepare you for each step; skipping its deadlines is the most common France mistake.

Can I work part-time in France?

Yes — around 964 hours a year (about 20 hours a week) on a student permit. Enough to help with living costs, especially outside Paris.
