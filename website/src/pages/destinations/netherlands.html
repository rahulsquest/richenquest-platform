the Netherlands FAQs

What families ask us about the Netherlands

What's the catch in the Netherlands?

Housing. Student accommodation is genuinely scarce in the big cities — we start the housing search with you the day you accept an offer, not after your visa.

How much does it cost?

Non-EU tuition typically €8,000–20,000 a year plus €900–1,400 a month living. No income-based discounts like Italy — budget honestly from the start.

Can I work part-time?

With a work permit arranged via the employer, up to 16 hours a week during term (or full-time in summer).
