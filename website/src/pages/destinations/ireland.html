Ireland FAQs

What families ask us about Ireland

How expensive is Ireland really?

Tuition of €10,000–25,000 plus Dublin's high rents make Ireland pricier than mainland Europe — but the English-language advantage and stay-back can justify it. We budget it honestly with you first.

Can I work while studying?

Yes — 20 hours a week during term and full-time in holidays on Stamp 2 conditions.

Is Ireland good for IT and data careers?

Its universities are strong in computing and analytics and industry recruits actively — though nobody can promise you a job, and we won't.
