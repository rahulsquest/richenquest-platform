Hungary FAQs

What families ask us about Hungary

Is Hungary's degree respected?

It's an EU degree with Bologna-standard recognition across Europe. Several Hungarian universities have long international traditions, especially in medicine and engineering.

What does living in Budapest cost?

Typically €500–900 a month all-in — among the most affordable European capitals for students.

When are scholarship deadlines?

Stipendium Hungaricum applications close early in the year (typically January) for autumn entry — start the previous autumn to be safe.
