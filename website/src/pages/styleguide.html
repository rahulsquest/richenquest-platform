Animation guidelines

  • Purposeful only: motion confirms an action or guides attention — never decoration for its own sake.
  • Fast: hovers/toggles 150ms (--duration-fast), most transitions 200ms, reveals 300ms max.
  • Cheap properties only: animate opacity, translate/transform, box-shadow — never layout properties (width, height, top).
  • Reduced motion is law: every animation collapses under prefers-reduced-motion (enforced globally in base.css; reveal.js exits early).
  • No scroll-jacking, no autoplaying carousels, no parallax.
