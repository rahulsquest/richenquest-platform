## 3. The finding

> ### Almost every segment resolves to Debrecen.
>
> **It is the only option in the largest Master's band, the only verified study-gap answer, one of
> only two no-IELTS routes, and half of Hungary-first.** If Debrecen's deadline moves, or it declines
> agent representation, **most of this matrix goes to zero simultaneously.**

That is not a portfolio. **It is one university with four accessories**, and the segment view makes
it undeniable in a way the university list never did.

**Second finding, equally uncomfortable:** the two countries this project has spent the most strategy
on — **Italy and Germany — have zero verified universities between them.** Files 42, 43 and 44 built
a detailed Italy thesis on top of an empty shelf.

## 5. Marketing must be rebuilt around segments *(Step 6)*

**The Debrecen content pack is university-first, and by this brief's standard that is the wrong
shape.** It should not be scrapped — every fact in it is verified and reusable — but it should be
re-cut:

| Current | Should become |
|---|---|
| *"Debrecen — February 2027"* | **"Master's in Europe under ₹15 lakh — the honest list"** |
| Reel A — *"No IELTS? Debrecen…"* | **"Europe without an IELTS certificate"** — Debrecen *and* Vistula, so the piece survives one of them changing |
| Reel B — *"Your 3-year gap…"* | **"Studying abroad after a study gap"** |
| Carousel — 8 Debrecen slides | **"February vs March vs September — which intake is actually yours"** |

**The commercial reason, not just the editorial one:** a segment-first asset **keeps working when a
university drops out.** A Debrecen-first asset dies the day Debrecen changes a rule — and with this
much concentration, that is a real exposure.

## 6. What I am not claiming

- **The ₹ figures are tuition only.** With living costs added, **Bachelor's ₹15–25 L probably empties too** — I cannot say so definitively because Hungarian living costs are disputed by a factor of two (File 54).
- **Segment counts are of *verified* universities.** The 15 unverified German and Irish records may well serve some of these segments. **They are invisible here for the same reason they are invisible to the matcher: nobody has checked them.**
