# File 64 — Verified Intelligence: the estimate that moved four times

**The single most important finding in this engagement, and it is about our own product.**

---

## 1. 🔴 Debrecen does not serve RichenQuest's core segment

**PRIMARY VERIFIED — the University of Debrecen's own cost-of-living page:**

> *"On average, students living and studying in Debrecen spend about **800 USD per month**."*
> Covering shared private accommodation, food, study materials and local transport. **Excluding
> tuition and unforeseen expenses.**

| Line | 2-year Master's |
|---|---|
| Tuition (USD 7,500–8,000/yr) | ₹13.05–13.92 L |
| Non-refundable + permit | ₹0.54 L |
| **Living — the university's own figure** | **₹16.70 L** |
| **TOTAL** | **₹30.29–31.16 L** |

**Still excluded and still unknown:** insurance, travel, translation, buffer.

> ### RichenQuest's only executable February product is a ₹30 lakh product, sold to a demographic defined as ₹10–25 lakh.

## 2. My estimate moved four times — always upward as sources improved

| File | Basis | Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| 60 | Hungarian national living averages | ₹24.5 L |
| 62 | **Assumed dormitory at HUF 40,000** | **₹15.89 L** ⬅ *my lowest, and my worst* |
| 63 | Private rental, from the university's accommodation page | ₹20.5–22.5 L |
| **64** | **The university's own published living figure** | **₹30.3–31.2 L** |

**Every time I got closer to a primary source, the number went up.**

> **That is the True Cost thesis (File 61) happening to my own estimates.** I argued that the industry
> systematically understates cost because it quotes tuition. **I then understated it four times myself
> — and each correction came from moving one step closer to the university's own words.**
>
> **If it happens to someone actively trying not to let it happen, it is not carelessness. It is the
> gravity of the industry**, and the only defence is refusing to publish a number without a primary
> source attached.

**Recorded as the failure reason for this cycle's self-critique**, per the brief.

## 3. Why the single confidence score failed — and what replaced it

**Debrecen carried `Confidence: Medium`. That single field averaged two facts that must never be
averaged:**

- Admissions facts — deadline, IELTS, gap policy, interview route — are **CONFIRMED, from the university's own pages.**
- Finance facts were **LOW**, resting on national averages and an assumed dorm rate.

**A "Medium" record let me publish ₹15.89 L feeling adequately hedged.** Per-dimension confidence
would have shown `Finance: LOW` next to that number and stopped it.

**Six fields added to Accounts** — the only system change this cycle, and justified by a failure that
actually happened rather than one I imagined:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Debrecen** | **CONFIRMED** | **LOW** | **UNKNOWN** | MEDIUM | DO NOT DISCUSS | 🔴 **NO — too expensive** |
| EU Business School | HIGH | MEDIUM | UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN | DO NOT DISCUSS | 🔴 NO |
| METU Budapest | MEDIUM | HIGH | UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN | DO NOT DISCUSS | 🔴 NO |
| Vistula | HIGH | **UNKNOWN** | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | DO NOT DISCUSS | ❓ UNKNOWN |
| Global College Malta | UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN | UNKNOWN | DO NOT DISCUSS | ❓ UNKNOWN |

> ### Four of five verified universities do NOT serve the core segment. The fifth is unknown because its tuition is unpublished.

## 4. What this means for the business

**This is not a research problem. It is a positioning problem, and it has been hiding under tuition
figures for the entire engagement.**

| Option | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **A. Keep the ₹10–25 L demographic, change the portfolio** | Requires universities we do not have. **Czechia at ₹11.7 L is the only candidate found, and it has zero universities researched** |
| **B. Keep the portfolio, change the demographic** | Debrecen/METU/EU serve **₹25–40 L** families. That is a real market — **but it is not the one RichenQuest was built to serve**, and it is the one IDP and AECC already own |
| **C. Split by intake** | **February = ₹25 L+ families** (the only executable product). **September = the core segment, via Italy and DSU**, which is what File 50 said the core segment's answer was |
| **D. Dormitory changes the answer** | If a dorm place is obtainable, Debrecen falls to **₹17.8–21.9 L** — back inside the band. **Worth up to ₹12.47 lakh per family and still unanswered** |

**C is the honest reconciliation, and it is what the calendar already implied.** File 50 established
Italy — the core segment's real answer — cannot serve February. **We have been trying to sell a
February product to a September demographic.**
