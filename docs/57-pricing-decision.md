# File 57 — The pricing decision, made decidable

**F1 has been open for eleven cycles. I have recommended "set the fee" twice, so I will not recommend
it a third time.** The rule says: find out *why* it has not happened, and solve that.

**The reason is that F1 was never decidable.** File 40 proposed ₹1,20,000 from reasoning, not
evidence. File 54 supplied the cost side. **Nobody supplied the market side.** Here it is.

---

## 1. What competitors actually charge

| Competitor | Model | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **IDP · AECC · KC Overseas** | **Free to the student** — paid commission by universities | **₹0** |
| **LeapScholar** | **Freemium.** Core counselling — profile evaluation, shortlisting, application guidance — is **free**. Monetised through **IELTS/PTE prep and Leap Finance loans** | **₹0** for counselling |
| **Yocket Premium** | Paid, end-to-end | **not published** |

*Source: LeapScholar's own 2026 fee guide for the industry band; competitor model descriptions from their public pages. Secondary — the band is a market description, not a quote.*

## 2. The finding that should change the decision

**File 43 §4 answered "why pay when IDP is free?" with the Italy/DSU argument:** RichenQuest is paid
by the student, so it recommends Italy where commission-funded agencies will not, and it does the
ISEE Parificato and DSU work they have no reason to do — worth €14–16k/year to the family.

**That argument is sound and it does not apply to February 2027.**

File 53 rejected CAF network and DSU execution as advantages: **zero CAFs contacted, never executed
once.** And File 50 established Italy cannot serve a February intake at all.

> ### For the February/March 2027 Hungary–Poland cohort, RichenQuest is selling admission and visa support — the exact service LeapScholar gives away free.
>
> **There is currently no verified differentiator that justifies a premium price for this intake.**
> The differentiators that do exist — verified data, publishing our own gaps, telling a family to wait
> for the right intake — are real (File 53) but they are **trust advantages, not €14,000 advantages.**

**This is the uncomfortable thing that has been sitting under F1 the whole time, and it is why the
decision felt unsafe to make.**

## 3. The decision this makes available

**Two prices, not one, because they are two different products.**

| | **February/March 2027** | **September 2027 (Italy/DSU)** |
|---|---|---|
| What is sold | Admission + visa execution | **ISEE Parificato + DSU management** + admission + visa |
| Competitor equivalent | **Free at Leap/IDP** | **Nobody offers it** |
| Value to the family | Time, accuracy, honesty | **€14–16k/year** |
| Defensible price | **Low — entry** | **High — File 43's ₹1.2–1.5L band** |

**Recommendation, with the reasoning exposed:**

- **February/March 2027: price low, deliberately.** This intake's purpose is getting from zero to one (File 56) — the first reviews and case studies. **A high price on an undifferentiated service against a free competitor will produce neither.**
- **September 2027: File 43's band applies**, *conditional on I2 closing.* If no CAF relationship exists by spring 2027, the DSU package does not exist, and the September price cannot be justified either.

**I am not naming a number.** Below ₹30,000 sits under the market floor and signals low quality; the
₹1.2–1.5L band belongs to a service that requires I2. **The February number is a founder judgement
between those two poles, and it is now a judgement with evidence on both sides rather than a blank.**

## 4. Financial honesty

| | |
|---|---|
| Expected cost of this decision | **₹0** |
| Expected revenue impact | **UNKNOWN.** Depends on a fee that does not exist and a conversion rate with no history |
| Expected time | One founder decision |
| Expected student impact | **Every student.** Nothing can be proposed, invoiced or collected until it is made |
| **Risk of deciding wrong** | **Low and reversible.** A first-ten-students price can be revised for student eleven |
| **Risk of not deciding** | **High.** No proposal, no payment, no revenue — and it compounds every week |

**The asymmetry is the argument: a wrong price is fixable, an absent price blocks everything.**

## 5. What was wrong in my previous thinking

**I framed F1 as a founder decision that was simply outstanding. It was not — it was under-specified,
and I supplied only half the inputs.**

More seriously: **I asserted across several files that RichenQuest's fee model is defensible because
of Italy and DSU, without checking that the argument survives contact with the intake we are actually
selling. It does not.** File 53 rejected the DSU capability as unproven, and I kept using the DSU
*argument* to justify pricing anyway. Those two positions were held simultaneously and they are
inconsistent.

**Corrected: the fee model is defensible for September 2027 if I2 closes. For February 2027 it is
defensible on trust and execution alone, which supports a modest price, not a premium one.**
