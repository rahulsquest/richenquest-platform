# File 55 — OKRs, Aug–Nov 2026

**Replacing the task backlog with objectives, as the founder asked.** I have stress-tested the four
proposed objectives rather than simply formatting them, because **an OKR set that cannot be hit
destroys the credibility of the system in month one** — and this is the first quarter RichenQuest
will run on one.

**Two of the proposed key results are not executable as written. One target is set by a calendar
nobody has checked. Those are corrected below, with the reasoning shown.**

---

## 1. The constraint nobody has priced in

**Objective 2's ceiling is not conversion. It is the November deadline.**

| Milestone | Date | Weeks away |
|---|---|---:|
| **Debrecen application deadline** *(⚠️ pending confirmation)* | **1 Nov 2026** | **11** |
| Debrecen last safe visa filing | 18 Nov 2026 | 13 |
| **Vistula spring close** *(2026-cycle basis)* | 10 Feb 2027 | **25** |
| Italy DSU window opens | 1 Jul 2027 | 45 |

**Working backwards from 1 November — how long does a family in Bihar take from first hearing about a
university to a submitted application?**

| Assumption | Last useful seminar |
|---|---|
| Aggressive — decides fast, documents ready | **18 Oct 2026** (9 weeks) |
| **Realistic** | **4 Oct 2026** (**7 weeks**) |
| Cautious — first-generation family, loan needed | **20 Sep 2026** (5 weeks) |

> ### There are roughly seven weeks of useful lead generation left for the February 2027 Debrecen intake. Not eleven.
>
> **After early October, every seminar should pitch Vistula's March 2027 intake (25 weeks of runway)
> and September 2027 — not February.** Selling a February start in late October means selling an
> intake the student cannot reach, which is the single most damaging thing a counsellor can do.

**This is the most useful output of this pass**, and it changes what the next seven weeks are for.

## 2. The objectives, corrected

### 🎯 O1 — Generate 100 qualified leads

**The only objective that can start today. Everything else is downstream of it.**

| KR | Proposed | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **KR1** | 10 seminars | ✅ **Keep.** ~7 before the February cut-off, the rest pitching March/September |
| **KR2** | "Sign 20 coaching institutes" | ⚠️ **Reframe: approach 20, host 6.** *"Sign"* implies a commercial agreement that does not need to exist and would slow every conversation down. A principal saying *"come and talk to our students"* is the whole ask |
| **KR3** | 30 referral leads | ✅ **Keep.** Templates written; 1,000+ past students never asked |
| **KR4** | "Publish 90 days of content" | 🔴 **Not executable as written.** The founder's own rule is *content only for READY universities*, and **there are zero.** Reframe: **publish for each university within 7 days of it reaching READY** — which makes KR4 a dependency on P14-1, not a content-production target |

**Definition of "qualified", so the number means something:** budget band stated · intended intake
stated · English status known · a real phone number. That is what `qualifyLead` scores, and it is
what separates 100 leads from 100 names.

### 🎯 O2 — Enrol students *(split by intake, because one number hides the constraint)*

## 4. Campaign tracker *(Priority 1's mandated fields)*

Copy this block per campaign. **The "Lessons learned" row is the only one that compounds.**

```
CAMPAIGN
  Name            e.g. "Seminar — <institute>, <date>"
  Goal            e.g. "25 capture slips"
  Audience        class/degree, budget band expected, intake
  Channel         seminar | referral | GBP | WhatsApp
  Cost            ₹ (travel, printing, time)
  Expected leads
  Actual leads
  Qualified       (of actual — budget + intake + English + phone)
  Counselling booked
  Applications
  Conversion      qualified ÷ actual
  Cost per lead   cost ÷ actual
  LESSONS LEARNED  what to change next time — one sentence, written same day
```

**Log every campaign as a Lead source in the CRM: `Lead_Source_Detail` = campaign name.** Without
that field the whole tracker is a spreadsheet nobody reconciles.

## 5. Seminar debrief *(Priority 3 — "nothing learned should be lost")*

**Within 24 hours of every seminar**, while it is still remembered:

| Capture | Feeds |
|---|---|
| **Every question asked** — verbatim | Student FAQ |
| **Every parent concern** — verbatim, in the words used | Parent FAQ |
| **Every objection**, and what answer worked | Objection handling |
| **Which slide lost the room** | Seminar kit |
| **Which slide got phones out** | Do more of it |
| Capture slips ÷ attendees | Campaign tracker |

**Rule: any question asked twice across two seminars becomes a knowledge article that week.** That is
how the knowledge base stops being written by me and starts being written by the market — which is
the only way it becomes accurate.

**A question nobody could answer on the spot is the most valuable output of a seminar.** It is a
verified gap in the counselling script, found for free.
