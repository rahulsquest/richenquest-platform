## 1. Competitive intelligence — verified figures

Researched 2026-08-15 from published sources.

### What the market actually charges

| Model | Fee | How it is funded |
|---|---|---|
| **Free counselling** — IDP, AECC, Leap Scholar (core) | **₹0 to the student** | **University commissions** |
| Paid, end-to-end | **₹30,000 – ₹1,50,000** | student pays |
| Yocket | ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000 | student pays |
| Bluesky Educational Services | ₹30,000 – ₹75,000 | student pays |
| Broad market range quoted | ₹15,000 – ₹2,00,000 | mixed |

**RichenQuest's standard fee is ₹1,80,000** (`claims.json`).

> ### That is above the top of the quoted paid range (₹30k–₹1.5L), while competing against free.

This is the single most commercially important finding in this document. It does not mean the price
is wrong — premium positioning is a real strategy — but it means **the price must be actively
justified in every conversation**, and right now there is no articulated reason why RichenQuest
costs 1.2–6× a paid competitor and infinitely more than a free one.

**Nobody has tested whether this fee converts.** Zero students have come through the platform. The
₹1,80,000 figure is a stated price, not a proven one.

### The competitive gap that is genuinely exploitable

The research names it directly:

> *"Free consultants are funded through university commissions, so their recommendations often lean
> toward partner institutions."*

**That is a structural conflict of interest, and it is RichenQuest's opening.** A student advised by
a free consultancy cannot easily tell whether the shortlist is right for *them* or profitable for
the *agency*.

A fee-paying model can honestly say: **"You pay us, so we work for you."** That is a real, defensible
difference — and it maps exactly onto the claims discipline already enforced in this platform
(`claims-guard`, no unverifiable statements, "we only make publicly verifiable claims").

### The strategic fork the founder must resolve

There is a tension that will not survive contact with reality if left unexamined:

```
Charge the student ₹1,80,000        →  independence is the product
Take university commission          →  the same conflict competitors have
Do both                             →  the independence claim weakens
```

Phase 3 of this mission pursues university commissions. Phase 1 sells a premium fee. **Both at full
strength is not coherent**, and a student who discovers you earn commission on the university you
recommended will not care about the distinction.

**Three coherent positions, pick one:**

| Position | Fee | Commission | Pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Independent advisor** | ₹1,50,000–1,80,000 | none, or disclosed and rebated | "We work for you, not for universities" |
| **B · Commission-funded** | ₹0–25,000 | primary income | Compete with IDP/AECC on service and locality |
| **C · Hybrid, disclosed** | ₹60,000–1,00,000 | taken and **openly disclosed** | "Lower fee because universities also pay us — and here is exactly who" |

**Recommendation: C.** A alone is hard at ₹1.8L with no track record and no brand. B puts you in
direct competition with IDP's scale and budget. **C prices inside the market's paid range, keeps
both revenue lines, and turns disclosure into the trust signal** — which is precisely the discipline
this company already runs on internally.

**This is a founder decision and it should be made before Wave 1 outreach**, because what you ask
universities for depends on it.

### Trust signals competitors use, and what RichenQuest can honestly claim

| Signal | Competitors | RichenQuest — verified position |
|---|---|---|
| Student volume | large claimed numbers | **1,000+ guided since 2024** — approved phrasing, verb must be *guided*, never *placed* |
| University partnerships | heavily advertised | **none signed** — cannot be claimed at all |
| Offices | multi-city | Patna, Bihar |
| Success/visa rates | commonly advertised | **not measured** — must not be claimed |
| Verified placements | rarely evidenced | **15**, with records |
| Reviews | Google/Trustpilot volume | **none yet — this is the biggest fixable gap** |

**Two honest assets competitors cannot easily copy:** genuine locality in Bihar/Jharkhand/eastern
UP/Nepal, where the large agencies are thin on the ground; and a company that demonstrably refuses
to overstate — which is unusual enough in this sector to be worth saying out loud.

## 2. Lead generation — the plan for the first 30 qualified leads

**Reminder of the constraint (File 37 §4): zero real leads have ever entered the system.** Lead
capture is built, verified and idle. This section is the only thing standing between the platform
and its first student.

### The cheapest lead source is already yours

`claims.json`: **1,000+ students guided since 2024.**

**That is an existing base of people who already know RichenQuest**, and it costs nothing to
contact. In this sector referral is the highest-converting channel by a wide margin — a
recommendation from someone who actually went abroad outweighs any advertisement.

**Action:** message past students and their families. Not a campaign — a personal note asking who
they know applying this intake. **This should produce the first 10 leads before any ad spend.**

### Channel plan, ranked by cost per qualified lead

| # | Channel | Cost | Speed | Why this rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Past-student referrals** (WhatsApp, personal) | ₹0 | days | Warmest possible; base already exists |
| **2** | **Google Business Profile** | ₹0 | 1–2 weeks | Highest-intent free channel. "study abroad consultant Patna" is a buying query. **Not yet claimed — do this first** |
| **3** | **Local coaching / IELTS centres** | revenue share | 2–4 weeks | They already have your exact audience mid-decision. Referral agreements, not ads |
| **4** | **WhatsApp broadcast + status** | ₹0 | days | The dominant channel in Bihar/Nepal. Not email |
| **5** | **YouTube, Hindi/Bhojpuri** | time | 1–3 months | Underserved: honest cost breakdowns for Germany/Ireland in Hindi. Compounds |
| **6** | **Facebook groups — Nepal student communities** | ₹0 | weeks | Nepali student groups are large and active. Answer questions; never spam |
| **7** | **Instagram** | time | 1–3 months | Right demographic, slower to convert |
| **8** | **Paid ads (Google/Meta)** | ₹ | fast | **Last, deliberately.** Do not buy traffic before conversion is known |

**Why paid ads are last:** without a measured lead→student rate you cannot compute an acceptable
cost per lead. Spending before that is buying data at the worst possible price. Get 30 organic
leads, measure conversion, *then* decide whether ads are viable.

### The 30-lead plan

| Weeks | Action | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claim & complete Google Business Profile. Message 50 past students/families | 10 leads |
| 2–3 | Approach 5 local coaching/IELTS centres. WhatsApp status + broadcast | 10 leads |
| 4–6 | 3 YouTube videos: honest cost of study in Germany / Ireland / visa basics, in Hindi. Answer questions in 3 Nepali FB groups | 10 leads |

**30 leads in 6 weeks, at effectively zero cash cost.** At the assumed 10% conversion that is ~3
students; the real value is that **it produces the conversion number** that makes every later
decision computable.

### Ask every lead for reviews later

The biggest fixable trust gap is **zero public reviews**. Every satisfied student should be asked
for a Google review. Ten genuine reviews outperform any amount of ad copy for a local business — and
it is free.

## 3. What must be measured from lead one

Do not wait for volume. From the very first lead, record:

| Field | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead source** | `Lead_Source_Detail` | Which channel actually works — the whole plan above is a hypothesis until this says otherwise |
| **Lead → consultation** | `Lead_Status` | First real funnel number |
| **Consultation → signed** | case `Stage` | The conversion that decides everything |
| **Actual fee agreed** | `Deals.Amount` | **₹1 crore is 56 students at ₹1.8L and 83 at ₹1.2L. Nobody knows which** |
| **Loss reason** | `Lost_Reason` | Mandatory on Closed Lost. If "Budget" dominates, §1's pricing question is answered empirically |

**The platform already captures all of these.** Nothing needs building — they simply need using.

`Lost_Reason` deserves emphasis: **if the first 20 losses are mostly "Budget", the ₹1,80,000 price
is the growth constraint**, and that is a finding worth more than any amount of marketing.
