# File 50 — Competitive advantage: the February 2027 decision

**Written as a COO memo, not an engineering note.** One question governs it: *will this make
RichenQuest win more students than competitors?*

**The answer changed the plan.** Priorities 1 and 2 of the brief — a February 2027 portfolio, and
Italy as the strategic advantage — turn out to be in direct conflict, and the research says so
plainly enough that pretending otherwise would cost a cohort.

---

## 1. The finding that reorders the year

> ### Italy cannot serve a February 2027 intake. It is a September 2027 market, and the reason is the scholarship calendar.

**The evidence.** Regional DSU scholarship calls (*bandi*) for an academic year are published between
**July and September**, and deadlines close **between late summer and early autumn**. Reported for
the 2026/27 cycle: **DSU Toscana closed 7 September 2026.** Deadlines differ by region.

**The arithmetic.** A student starting **February 2027** sits in the **2026/27 academic year**. The
DSU deadline for that year closes **around September 2026 — now.** That student has no offer yet, no
ISEE Parificato, and in most cases has not chosen a region. **They cannot apply.**

**Why that is fatal rather than inconvenient.** DSU is worth **€14,000–16,000 a year** and it is the
entire reason RichenQuest recommends Italy (File 42). A February placement means arriving **without
it** — which converts Italy from the most affordable destination available to a low-income family
into an ordinary one, at full price, in a country whose undergraduate teaching is mostly in Italian.

**A second, independent reason.** Most Italian **undergraduate** programmes are taught in Italian;
English-taught provision concentrates at Master's level, and availability differs markedly between
the September and February intakes. **The February pool is thin before the scholarship problem is
even considered.**

### The commercial move this unlocks

This is not a setback. **It is the most persuasive conversation RichenQuest can have:**

> *"Italy is the best value we work with, and that is because of a regional scholarship worth
> €14,000–16,000 a year. Applications for it close in early autumn. Start now for September 2027 and
> you can apply properly. Rush into February and you go without it — and then Italy stops being the
> cheap option."*

**That converts better than a February placement would, because it is visibly against our own
short-term interest.** A commission-funded competitor cannot say it — they are paid on enrolment, so
"wait seven months" costs them the fee. **This is the differentiator, spoken out loud.**

### What it means operationally

**Italy work is not deprioritised — it is re-timed.** The deadline that matters is not a university's;
it is the **July–September 2027 DSU window**. Everything that must exist before it:

| Must be in place before July 2027 | Status |
|---|---|
| **CAF relationship for ISEE Parificato** | 🔴 **I2 — founder negotiation.** Now has a hard date, not a vague priority |
| Regional DSU deadline calendar | build once, reuse annually — **cannot be built accurately until the 2027/28 bandi publish** |
| ISEE threshold per region | 2026 reference ≈ **€26,306**, and the required document is the **university ISEE**, not the ordinary one. **Re-verify annually** |
| Universitaly familiarity | operational |

**I2 has been open across five missions as a priority with no deadline. It now has one: July 2027,
working backwards means a CAF conversation started by spring.**

### Vistula — verified this pass, and the honest picture

**Verified from `vistula.edu.pl`:**

- **English B2** — IELTS 5.5 / TOEFL iBT 72, **and an MOI waiver**: a student whose prior education was in English may apply **with no certificate**.
- **⚠️ The waiver's condition, which must be said out loud every time:** on arrival they must pass the University's own English test, and **if they fail, a PREP course of at least one semester.** A student who hears only *"no IELTS needed"*, arrives, fails, and loses a semester will blame RichenQuest — correctly.
- **Offer turnaround ~2–3 days.** The fastest in the portfolio by a wide margin, and a genuine advantage for late deciders.
- Scholarships: social PLN 1,000–1,600/month, Rector's PLN 450, disability, incentive grants to PLN 8,000.
- Holds **official MSWiA authorisation** for international student recruitment.

**Tuition is NOT verified and must not be quoted.** Vistula's own fee page publishes no figures —
they sit behind the application portal. Secondary sources suggest **~€3,000–4,500/yr Bachelor's**,
which would make it the **cheapest option in the portfolio** — and that is exactly why it cannot be
repeated. **An unverified low price is the most damaging number a counsellor can say**, because the
family budgets on it.

> **The gap is the opportunity.** Requesting the official fee schedule is the natural opening of the
> partnership conversation, and `cooperation@vistula.edu.pl` is already verified. **One email does
> double duty: it closes the data gap and starts the relationship.**

**And it corrects the record:** Vistula is **March**, not February. For a family that is the same
decision and the same cycle. For a campaign it is a different month, and advertising it as February
would be a small lie that a student discovers at the worst moment.

### On "15–20 flagship universities"

**RichenQuest has 4 with any verified data and 16 with none.** The brief says maintain 15–20 and do
not keep dead inventory. **Those two instructions currently conflict, and "no dead inventory" wins.**

> **A portfolio of 20 universities where 16 are unverified is not a portfolio of 20. It is a
> portfolio of 4 and a list of 16 leads.** The matcher already treats it that way.

**Target: 8–10 genuinely verified by the February cycle, not 20 nominal.** Verification is the
constraint, and each one is a real research pass.

## 3. Competitor positioning — the recommendation, not a survey

File 43 §4 already carries the competitor comparison. **What this pass adds is the sharpened position
the Italy finding hands us.**

| | Commission-funded (IDP, AECC, KC Overseas) | Student-paid (Yocket, Leap, SI-UK) | **RichenQuest** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid by | universities | student | student |
| Can they say *"wait seven months for the scholarship"*? | **No — the fee dies with the deferral** | Partly | **Yes** |
| Incentive on low-commission Italy | **weak** | moderate | **aligned** |
| Publish what they have **not** verified | no | no | **yes** |
| Brand, reviews, scale | **far stronger** | **stronger** | **weak — 0 reviews** |

**The positioning, in one line RichenQuest can defend and no competitor can copy without changing
their business model:**

> **"We are paid by you, so we will tell you to wait when waiting is worth €14,000 a year — and we
> will tell you what we haven't checked."**

**Both halves matter.** The first is structurally unavailable to a commission agency. The second is
available to anyone and taken by nobody, because it requires publishing your own gaps — which is
what `Verified_On`, `Source_URL`, `Confidence` and `Review_Date` on every university record now make
possible as a habit rather than a claim.

**Not copying anyone.** The one thing worth learning from the aggregators is operational speed, and
Vistula's 2–3 day offer turnaround is the closest thing in this portfolio to matching it.
