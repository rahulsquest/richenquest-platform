# File 61 — The problem nobody in India solves: True Cost

**"What can RichenQuest become the best in India at?"**

The answer has been accumulating in my own research for four cycles without my naming it.

---

## 1. The problem

**Every consultancy in India quotes tuition. Nobody computes what the degree actually costs.**

This is not a claim about competitors' honesty. It is what my own research kept discovering, each time
by accident:

| What the brochure says | What it actually costs | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Debrecen Master's — **₹10.5 L** *(tuition)* | **₹24.5 L** with living | **2.3×** |
| METU February Bachelor's — *"an affordable option"* | **~₹30 L** with living | — |
| Debrecen application — *"USD 150 fee"* | **USD 500** — application **+ entrance exam**, both non-refundable, **before any decision** | 3.3× |
| Germany — *"€11,904, the wall"* | **Not a cost.** It is the student's own living money at €992/month | **misrepresented entirely** |
| Poland — *"students can work part-time"* | **Since 1 Dec 2025 most universities need a work permit** | **advice is stale industry-wide** |
| *"Bachelor's in Europe from ₹10 lakh"* | **Does not exist.** Living costs alone are ₹10–22 L over three years | **the claim is arithmetically false** |

**Every one of those gaps is discoverable in an afternoon. Nobody discloses them** — and the reason is
structural, not moral.

## 2. Why nobody solves it — and why that is the moat

> **A commission-funded agency cannot publish True Cost, because True Cost makes every university look
> expensive and reduces enrolments. They are paid on enrolment.**

**RichenQuest is paid by the family. Honest cost disclosure *increases* trust, which increases
conversion.** The incentive runs the opposite way — and that is the definition of a structural
advantage rather than a marketing angle.

**This is the same root cause as every other proven advantage** in File 53: *being paid by the student
lets RichenQuest say things that cost it money.* True Cost is the sharpest, most demonstrable
expression of it, and unlike DSU expertise **it requires no CAF, no partner and no permission — it
exists today.**

**And it is testable by the family.** A parent can check a tuition figure against a university's own
site in five minutes. **A company that publishes a number that survives that check, including the
lines it says it does not know, has earned something no advertisement buys.**

## 3. The product: the RichenQuest True Cost Report

**One document, given free in the first counselling session, before any fee is discussed.**

### Non-negotiable design rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Every line is VERIFIED, UNKNOWN, or DISPUTED — never blank** | A blank line reads as zero. `UNKNOWN` is information; a gap is a lie by omission |
| **Every verified line carries its source and the date checked** | The parent can check it. That is the point |
| **Non-refundable money is a separate, highlighted block** | It is the money they lose if the answer is no |
| **The total is labelled a FLOOR** while any line is UNKNOWN | Never *"the total cost"* |
| **Part-time earnings appear as a RISK BUFFER, never as a deduction from the requirement** | File 60. The family must fund the gross |
| **No scholarship is deducted from the total** | Availability is not an award |

```
   Per year × years                     ____   [VERIFIED / DISPUTED / UNKNOWN]

```

### Worked example — Debrecen, 2-year Master's, from records verified 16 Aug 2026

| | | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Non-refundable before any decision** | **USD 500** — USD 150 application + USD 350 entrance exam | ✅ application fee verified from `edu.unideb.hu`; exam fee secondary |
| B. Tuition | USD 7,500–8,000/yr × 2 = **~₹14 L** | ✅ |
| C. Living | **~₹11 L** over 2 years | ⚠️ **DISPUTED 2×** — sources give €6,000 and €7,700/yr |
| D. One-off | visa · insurance · permit · flight · buffer | 🔴 **UNKNOWN** |
| **FLOOR** | **≈ ₹24.5 L** | **before D** |
| E. Could reduce | merit 20% **from Year 2** · alumni 10% · sibling 10% · Hungarian work rules **not researched** | availability only |

**The single most valuable line in that table is C.** Every competitor states a Hungarian living cost
with confidence. **We say the sources disagree by a factor of two and we will confirm it with the
consulate for your intake.** A parent who has been given three confident different numbers by three
agencies recognises that sentence immediately.

## 4. What this changes

| Area | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Counselling** | Starts with a university shortlist | **Starts with the True Cost Report.** The shortlist follows from what the family can actually fund |
| **Marketing** | *"Study in Hungary from ₹10 lakh"* | **"What studying in Europe actually costs — including what we haven't verified"** |
| **Seminar** | Slide 4 already carries the *"we have not verified living costs"* line | **Make it the whole talk.** It is the strongest 30 seconds in the deck |
| **Pricing (F1)** | *"Why pay when IDP is free?"* had no February answer | **This is the February answer.** Not DSU — that is September. **True Cost is what the fee buys now** |
| **Knowledge base** | Organised by university | **Reorganise by problem** — cost, no-IELTS, study gap, scholarships, intake timing |

**Point 4 matters most.** File 57 established that for February 2027 we sell what LeapScholar gives
away free, and that this argued for a low price. **True Cost is a genuine differentiator that exists
today** — it does not make the fee premium, but it makes it defensible, which the February campaign
did not previously have.

## 6. Honest limits

- **This is a product design, not a delivered product.** It becomes real the first time a counsellor hands one to a family.
- **It makes universities look expensive.** Some families will walk away — **and the ones who stay will be fundable, which is the point.** A student who enrols on a number that was wrong becomes a crisis in month nine.
- **It raises the standard we must then meet.** Publishing "verified on this date" means re-verifying every intake. `readinessSweep` and `Review_Date` already enforce that, which is the one place the internal system genuinely earns its keep.
