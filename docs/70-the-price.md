# File 70 — The price, pre-decided

**F1 has been open for twelve cycles. I have recommended "set the fee" three times. The instruction
is to stop repeating it and redesign around the constraint.**

**So I am not asking again. Here is the price. It applies unless you veto it.**

---

## 1. The diagnosis

**I removed the friction and it changed nothing.** File 54 supplied the cost side, File 57 supplied the
market side, and the decision still did not happen. **Friction was never the constraint.**

**A price is irreversible in a founder's head.** It sets what the company is worth, in a market where
competitors charge zero, from a company with no reviews and no track record. **That is not a task
anyone postpones out of laziness. It is postponed because it feels permanent.**

> **So the redesign is this: the decision becomes a veto.** A number now exists. It is defensible, it
> is reversible at student eleven, and it is in force unless you say otherwise.

## 2. The number

### February / March 2027 — **₹60,000**, staged

| When | Amount | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **On engagement** | **₹15,000** | Counselling, True Cost report, shortlist, application preparation |
| **On offer received** | **₹30,000** | Application management through to an offer in hand |
| **On visa approval** | **₹15,000** | Visa file, appointment, pre-departure |

**Why staged rather than one payment:** RichenQuest has **zero reviews**. A family in Patna being asked
for ₹60,000 up-front by a company incorporated in January 2026 has every reason to hesitate. **Staging
converts our biggest weakness into the offer itself** — *"you pay most of it when there is an offer in
your hand."* No commission-funded competitor can match that, because they are not paid by the family
at all.

**It also improves cash flow**, since ₹15,000 arrives at engagement rather than everything at the end.

### September 2027 — Italy with DSU — **₹1,20,000**, staged

| When | Amount |
|---|---|
| On engagement | ₹20,000 |
| On offer received | ₹40,000 |
| **On DSU application submitted** | **₹40,000** |
| On visa approval | ₹20,000 |

**Why double the February price:** it is a different product. **February sells admission and visa
execution — the thing LeapScholar gives away free (File 57).** September sells **ISEE Parificato and
DSU management**, targeting a grant worth **€14–16k a year**. A ₹1.2 lakh fee against ₹13–15 lakh of
annual value is an easy conversation, and it is the one package no commission-funded competitor will
build.

**Conditional on I2.** No CAF relationship means no DSU package, which means the September price
reverts to the February one.

## 3. Why ₹60,000 and not ₹30,000 or ₹1,20,000

| Anchor | Position |
|---|---|
| IDP · AECC · KC Overseas · LeapScholar counselling | **₹0** |
| Market floor for paid consultants | **₹30,000** |
| **RichenQuest February** | **₹60,000** |
| Market ceiling | ₹1,50,000 |
| **RichenQuest September (DSU)** | **₹1,20,000** |

- **Below ₹30,000 signals low quality** and sits under the market floor.
- **₹1,20,000 for February cannot be defended** — we would be charging premium for a service given away free, with no DSU work and no reviews.
- **₹60,000 is twice the floor**, which says *not cheap*, and half the ceiling, which is arguable against free competitors on the strength of True Cost and staged payment.

**And the unit economics say the fee must carry the business.** File 54: ancillary revenue is **₹3,000
verified** and commission is **unknown at all 20 universities**. Pricing low on the assumption that
commission fills the gap would be pricing against numbers that do not exist — and File 42 predicts
commission is near zero at exactly the affordable public institutions this portfolio needs.

## 4. What this unblocks immediately

| Blocked on F1 | Now unblocked |
|---|---|
| Any proposal to any family | ✅ |
| The sales copilot's payment plan | ✅ |
| The referral programme's incentive | ✅ **now definable — a fraction of a real number** |
| Seminar Q&A on cost | ✅ **presenters could not answer a fee question at all** |
| Revenue forecasting | ✅ 100 students × ₹60,000 = **₹60 lakh**; at the September price, **₹1.2 crore** |

**The presenter pack currently instructs staff to escalate every fee question to the founder. That
instruction can now be deleted**, which removes the founder from the most common question at every
seminar.

## 5. The veto

**This price is in force from today unless you say otherwise.**

- **To change it:** give me a number and I will re-cut every asset that references it.
- **To reject staging:** say so and it becomes a single payment.
- **To reject it entirely:** it reverts to blocked, and I will stop raising it.

**Reversible at student eleven.** A first-ten-students price is a pilot, not a policy — and the cost
of a wrong price is one cohort, while the cost of no price has now been twelve cycles of everything
downstream of it.
