# File 62 — True Cost Report: Hungary

**One country, verified deeply, rather than five researched shallowly.** Hungary was chosen because
Debrecen is the only path RichenQuest can execute today, and because its living cost was the figure
that decided whether our February product costs ₹19 L or ₹24.5 L.

**It turned out to be neither, and the reason is a variable nobody discusses.**

---

## 1. The finding: the dormitory is the whole argument

| Accommodation | Per month | Over a 2-year Master's |
|---|---|---|
| **University dormitory, 3-bed** | **HUF 40,000** | **₹2.30 L** |
| University dormitory, newer rooms | HUF 47,600 | ₹2.74 L |
| **Private rental** | **HUF 120,000–140,000** | **₹8.06 L** |

> ### The accommodation choice alone swings the total by ₹5.76 lakh — more than a third of the tuition.
>
> **No consultancy discusses this, and it is the single largest controllable variable in the entire
> cost stack.** A student who secures a dorm place and one who does not are having two different
> financial experiences of the same degree.

**This also corrects my own File 60 figure.** I had been carrying Hungarian living costs at
€6,000–7,700/yr from national averages. **For a Debrecen student in a dormitory that is materially
overstated** — Debrecen is a small city, and HUF 40,000/month is roughly €100.

## 3. The disputed line, and why the dispute exists

**I could not resolve the Hungarian subsistence figure, and I found out why.**

The official source — `oif.gov.hu` — requires the applicant to show resources covering *"subsistence,
accommodation, return/onward travel and healthcare"* and **states no monetary amount at all.**

> **The secondary sources disagree by a factor of two (HUF 200,000 vs HUF 98,000/month) because the
> authority does not publish a number. They are each guessing, confidently.**

**That is a more useful answer than a figure would have been**, and it is exactly what the brief's
quality rule asks for: *if sources disagree, mark DISPUTED and explain why.*

**What a counsellor says:** *"Hungary's immigration authority does not publish a fixed amount. It
requires you to show you can cover living, accommodation, travel and healthcare. We confirm the
figure the consulate expects for your intake, because agencies quoting you a precise number are
quoting something the government has not published."*

## 4. The three totals

**Never one number.**

| | Total | What it assumes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum Verified Cost** | **₹15.89 L** | Cheapest tuition, **dorm place secured at HUF 40,000** |
| **Likely Cost** | **₹16.77 L** | Mid tuition, newer dorm room |
| **Maximum Expected Cost** | **₹22.52 L** | Top tuition, **no dorm place — private rental** |

> ### ⚠️ All three are FLOORS.
> Food, transport, insurance, travel, translation and buffer are **UNKNOWN** and therefore **excluded,
> not estimated.** The real number is higher than every figure above and we will not pretend otherwise.

**What this changes commercially:** File 60 put Debrecen at ₹24.5 L and concluded it was 2× the
cheapest path. **With the dorm verified, the minimum is ₹15.89 L** — much closer to Poland's ₹13.9 L
and inside the ₹15–25 L segment rather than above it.

**Debrecen is more affordable than I reported last cycle, conditional on one thing: securing a
dormitory place.** That condition is now the most important operational question to ask the
university — and it has been added to its email.

## 5. Trust impact

**The line a parent will remember is not a number. It is §3** — *"the government does not publish this
figure, so anyone quoting you a precise one is guessing."*

**That sentence is verifiable, useful, and impossible for a commission-funded agency to say**, because
it undermines the confident quote their own counsellor just gave. **It is the True Cost product
working exactly as File 61 predicted.**
