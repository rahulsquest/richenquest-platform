## 1. The fact that changes the commercial case

**PRIMARY — ER.GO (Emilia-Romagna) bando:**

> *"Per gli studenti internazionali con nucleo familiare non residente in Italia, il valore della
> borsa di studio è riconosciuto nell'importo previsto per gli studenti **Fuori Sede**,
> indipendentemente dal luogo di residenza o domicilio."*

**An international student whose family is not resident in Italy is automatically awarded the
*fuori sede* rate — the highest band — regardless of where they actually live.**

**They do not have to prove they moved away from home. Being Indian is the qualification.**

**And the amount is nationally floored:** the minimum *fuori sede* amount for **AY 2026/27 is
€7,171.11** — confirmed independently in both the Emilia-Romagna and Piemonte sources.

## 2. What that does to the numbers

| 2-year Master's | Total |
|---|---|
| **Debrecen** *(university's own living figure)* | **₹30.3–31.2 L** |
| Poland | ₹13.9 L |
| Czechia | ₹11.7 L |
| **Italy WITHOUT DSU** | ₹13.1–24.7 L |
| **Italy WITH DSU** | **₹0–3.5 L + fees** |

**With DSU: tuition waived, €7,171/year cash, plus accommodation and meal benefits on top.**

> ### An Italian Master's with DSU can cost a family under ₹5 lakh. Debrecen costs ₹30 lakh.
>
> **That is not a better option. It is a different category of outcome**, and it is available
> precisely to the low-income families RichenQuest was built to serve.

**The mandatory caveats, which must travel with the number every time:**

1. **DSU is not guaranteed.** It is applied for and awarded against ISEE thresholds and available funding.
2. **The family must fund the gross cost anyway** — the scholarship arrives after enrolment, not before the visa.
3. **The visa requires proof of funds regardless.** Italy accepts loans and scholarships as proof (File 43), but the funds question comes first.
4. **No student may ever be told they will get DSU.**

## 3. 🔁 This corrects Workstream 1's premise

**The brief says: select universities by DSU generosity.** The research says **DSU cash is largely
nationally standardised** — €7,171.11 is a national floor, and the international *fuori sede* rule
applies broadly.

> **So selecting universities by "DSU generosity" would be optimising a variable that barely varies.**

**What genuinely varies between regions, and should drive selection instead:**

| Variable | Why it decides the outcome |
|---|---|
| **Accommodation capacity** | DSU housing is **not guaranteed even to winners** (File 43). A region that houses its winners is worth more than one paying slightly more cash |
| **ISEE threshold** | ER.GO **€25,000** · Piemonte ~**€26,306**. Small differences, but they decide eligibility at the margin |
| **Cost of living** | The gap between the €7,171 cash and actual living cost. **Bologna and Turin are cheaper than Milan or Rome** |
| **English-taught Master's supply** | Concentrated at Master's level; **most Italian Bachelor's are taught in Italian** |
| **Application window** | Piemonte 2026/27 ran **22 Jul – 4 Sep 2026**. Each region differs, and missing it costs a year |

## 4. Verified regional intelligence

| | **ER.GO — Emilia-Romagna** | **EDISU — Piemonte** |
|---|---|---|
| **Fuori sede cash** | up to **€7,171/yr** | **€7,171.11** national minimum |
| **ISEE ceiling** | **€25,000** (ISPE €50,000) | ~**€26,306** |
| Max-award ISEE band | ≤ **€16,666.67** | — |
| **2026/27 window** | — | **22 Jul – 4 Sep 2026, 12:00** |
| **International access** | **Auto *fuori sede* rate** | **Codice Temporaneo d'Accesso** in place of SPID/CIE |
| **Document route** | ISEE universitario | **ISEE universitario OR complete consular documentation** |

**The last row is operationally significant and previously unknown to us.** Piemonte's own materials
indicate international students may present **complete consular documentation** as an alternative to
the ISEE universitario. **If that holds, it is a partial route around the ISEE Parificato bottleneck
that File 43 called the single most common reason a qualifying student loses DSU.**

⚠️ **NEEDS CONFIRMATION from the bando text itself.** It is the highest-value verification in the
Italy engine — **it could reduce dependence on I2, the CAF relationship that has blocked Italy since
File 42.**
