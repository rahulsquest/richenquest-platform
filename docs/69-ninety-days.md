## 4. Engine 1 — executed, not designed

**The brief asks for a system that turns every verified fact into eleven formats.** Building that
pipeline before publishing anything once would be automating an empty process.

**So instead: the first artefact exists.** `marketing/ARTICLE-what-a-europe-masters-actually-costs.md`
— the verified corpus as one publishable piece. Its content is the flywheel:

- The ₹13 lakh tuition versus the **₹30 lakh total, from the university's own page**
- The **₹43,000 non-refundable** before any decision
- Germany's blocked account explained as **liquidity, not cost**
- **Poland's work-permit change** the industry has not updated for
- Italy's **fuori sede** rule — and three warnings against believing it too easily
- Hungary's subsistence figure — **"there is no published number to agree on"**
- **"We have corrected our own estimate four times, and each time the real number went up"**

**The other ten formats are the same text, re-cut.** Carousel, WhatsApp, parent PDF, seminar slide —
**no new evidence, no new claims.** That is the rule, and it is what makes an eleven-format pipeline
safe to build later rather than now.

## 6. Engine 4 — learning, at the only scale that matters now

**Automating a learning engine for zero students would be building a mill with no grain.** For the
next 100 students, learning is two rules already written:

1. **Seminar debrief within 24 hours** — every question verbatim (`WAR-ROOM-7-WEEKS.md` §4)
2. **Any question asked twice becomes a knowledge article that week**

**That is the whole learning engine until ~100 students.** It requires no software, and it is how the
knowledge base stops being written by me and starts being written by the market.
