# File 72 — The RichenQuest Revenue Engine

**The company's operating philosophy for the next five years.**

---

## 1. The assumption I am deleting

**File 54 concluded: "the fee is essentially the entire business."** That was true of the data I had
and wrong as a philosophy, and it has quietly shaped every document since.

**It models a transaction. RichenQuest's asset is a relationship that lasts two to five years.**

> ### A student does not stop needing us when they land. They stop hearing from us — because every consultancy in India is paid at enrolment and has no reason to call again.

## 2. The three recurring services nobody in India offers

**These are not upsells. They are legally mandatory or financially protective, they recur annually,
and no commission-funded agency will ever build them** — because their revenue event happened
eighteen months earlier.

| Service | Why it recurs | What it protects |
|---|---|---|
| **Residence permit renewal** | **Annual and legally mandatory.** Italy, Hungary, Poland — every year, every student | **Their legal right to remain.** Miss it and the degree stops |
| **DSU renewal / credit compliance** *(Italy)* | **Annual.** Maintenance requirements apply from year two | **€14–16k a year.** A student short on credits loses the grant that made Italy possible |
| Insurance renewal | Annual, required for the permit | Permit eligibility |

**The second one is the most valuable service this company could ever sell.** A student who misses
their credit threshold in year two loses roughly **₹13 lakh of grant**. Charging **₹15,000** to
prevent that is not a fee — it is the cheapest insurance in their life.

⚠️ **Before selling it, the credit-maintenance rule must be verified per region.** File 43 recorded it
as *"applies from the second year — verify per region"* and it has never been confirmed. **A service
line resting on an unverified rule is the exact error this company exists to avoid** — this is now the
highest-value verification on the board.

## 4. Why this beats chasing forex and loans

**File 67 argued the money is in financial services. I still think that is where the ceiling is — but
it is the wrong thing to build next, and the reason is not sequencing.**

| | Financial services | Recurring student services |
|---|---|---|
| Requires a partner | ✅ **and we have zero leverage at zero students** | 🔴 **none** |
| Requires a licence | sometimes | no |
| Margin | shared with the partner | **entirely ours** |
| Increases trust | **neutral to negative** — it looks like we're selling | **strongly positive** — we are protecting them |
| Competitors will copy | eventually, yes | **they structurally cannot** |

> **Selling a family a forex rate makes us a broker. Keeping their permit valid and their scholarship
> intact makes us their institution.** Only one of those two generates the referral.

## 5. Premium services — the Part 3 test

**Must satisfy all five: students pay willingly · competitors ignore · AI cannot replace · universities
do not provide · free consultancies will not build.**

| Service | Passes? |
|---|---|
| **DSU credit-compliance monitoring** | ✅ **all five.** Universities won't chase you, AI can't sit in a registrar's office, free agencies were paid two years ago |
| **Residence permit renewal** | ✅ all five |
| **Clarity Report** (₹2,500, credited) | ✅ — a free consultancy *cannot* build it; it argues families out of purchases |
| **Parent quarterly briefing** | ✅ — **the buyer is the parent, and nobody has ever sold to them directly** |
| Accommodation search | 🔴 AI and marketplaces do it better |
| Test prep | 🔴 crowded, commoditised |
| Generic "career services" | 🔴 we have no employer network and no right to claim one |

> ⚠️ **AMENDED BY FILE 73: the parent briefing requires the student's written consent, and the
> student sees every report before it is sent.** The student is an adult; reporting on them without
> consent makes us the parents' informant.

**The parent briefing is the one I had not seen.** The parent pays for the degree, carries the
anxiety, and is never the customer of anything. **A structured quarterly report — attendance, permit
status, exams passed, money spent versus planned — is a product with a willing buyer and near-zero
delivery cost.** It also produces the review and the referral, because the person who tells the
neighbours is the mother, not the student.

## 6. Bottlenecks — only the next stage

**Current stage: 0 → 100 students. The only bottleneck is leads.**

| Stage | What breaks | Act now? |
|---|---|---|
| **0 → 100** | **Leads.** Nothing else has ever been the constraint | ✅ **the only thing** |
| 100 → 500 | Counsellor capacity — ~2–3 full-time counsellors per 100 students at current effort | 🔴 **two years away. Ignore** |
| 500 → 1,000 | Training and quality consistency | 🔴 ignore |
| 1,000+ | Everything else | 🔴 ignore |

**The rule is not "plan ahead". It is: solve the bottleneck you are standing in front of.** Every
system built for stage three while stuck at stage one is the mistake this project already made with
the CRM.

**The only unprotected threat is the one this file introduces**, and it is the largest: **a student who
stops hearing from us stops referring us.**

**Number 10 is not a vanity metric.** A month with no corrections means either we published nothing or
we stopped verifying. **Both are the same failure**, and it is the failure that ends the company.

## 10. The philosophy, in five lines

1. **Charge for what we control. Never for what we do not.**
2. **The fee buys the relationship. The relationship is the business.**
3. **Recurring services beat referral commissions**, because protecting a student builds trust and brokering to them spends it.
4. **Solve the bottleneck in front of you.** Everything built for a stage you are not in is waste.
5. **Publish the corrections.** The month we stop is the month the moat starts closing.
