## Week 1 — Foundation (Days 1–5)

### Day 1: Zoho One activation
1. Go to zoho.com/one → sign in with the account holding your credits → subscribe to **All-Employee plan**, India data center (it asks during signup — choose India).
2. Open **Zoho Directory** (one.zoho.com → Admin Panel): add every employee with their work email. Create three groups: `Management`, `Counselors`, `Operations`, plus `Finance`.
3. Turn on **two-factor authentication** requirement (Directory → Security Policies). Non-negotiable — you'll hold passports and financial documents.

### Day 2: Email + Chat
1. **Zoho Mail:** Admin Console → add your domain → it shows DNS records (MX, SPF, DKIM). Add these in your domain registrar's DNS panel. (Send me a screenshot of your registrar's DNS page and I'll tell you exactly what to paste where.)
2. **Zoho Cliq:** create channels — `#announcements`, `#leads`, `#operations`, `#finance-approvals`, `#wins` (post every signed client & visa approval here — culture matters). One channel per team.

### Day 3–4: CRM core build
Open **file 01** and build the CRM exactly as specced: custom fields, pipeline stages, then the automations. Budget 3–4 hours. This is the single most important build in the whole system — everything else plugs into it.

### Day 5: WorkDrive + Vault
1. **WorkDrive:** create Team Folder `Students` (structure in file 01, section 6).
2. **Vault:** store every shared login (university portals, social accounts) — then change those passwords so they exist *only* in Vault.

1. Open founder dashboard — I've defined the 7 numbers to check (file 01 §8).
