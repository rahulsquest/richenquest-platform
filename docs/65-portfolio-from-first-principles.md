## 7. Fastest paths

**To 100 students:** **~5 in February 2027 · ~95 in September 2027.** Which means the next nine
months are a **September build**, not a February campaign. **Italy and Czechia researched by
November 2026, marketed December–April, applications by May, DSU July–September.**

**To ₹1 crore:** 100 students × a fee that does not exist. **At File 40's ₹1.2 L that is ₹1.2 crore —
so 100 students IS the ₹1 crore plan**, and F1 is the only thing between the two numbers.

**To trust:** already identified and already in hand — **True Cost** (File 61). It needs no partner,
no licence and no permission. **It is the only competitive asset RichenQuest owns outright today.**

## 8. Biggest strategic opportunity

> **Italy, for a demographic nobody else serves, with a scholarship nobody else bothers to chase.**

DSU is worth **€14–16k/year**, is means-tested on family income rather than merit alone, and is
**structurally unattractive to commission-funded agencies** because Italian public universities pay
them almost nothing. **RichenQuest's students — low-income families in Bihar and Nepal — are exactly
the profile DSU is designed for.**

**It is the only place where the core demographic, the fee model, the competitive gap and the
scholarship all align.** Every other position in the portfolio is a supporting act.

**It requires I2 — the CAF relationship — and I2 has been open since File 42 with no date. It now has
one: spring 2027, or Italy misses another year.**
