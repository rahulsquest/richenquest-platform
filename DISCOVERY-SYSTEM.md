# How strangers find RichenQuest without Rahul

**Test: he disappears for 30 days. The next 100 families still discover the company.**

**The design rule under every engine below:** *a mechanism must be triggered by a student's own
progress or by a fixed date — never by someone remembering to do marketing.* **A campaign needs a
person. A trigger does not.**

---

## 1. DISCOVERY ENGINE

**The Numbered Report.** Report No. 1 exists. **The system is that there is a No. 2, and a No. 3, on a
fixed date.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | One cost report, quarterly, numbered and dated. Each covers one destination or one question families are being misled about |
| **Why it runs without Rahul** | **A recurring publication is self-justifying outreach.** A node can be messaged because Report No. 2 exists — nobody has to invent an occasion, which is the thing that requires a founder |
| **Why anyone shares it** | It makes the sharer look useful and costs them nothing. **There is nothing to buy in it** |
| **Why they remember us** | **Numbered things are remembered as a series.** "The people who publish the cost reports" is an identity; "a consultancy in Patna" is not |
| **How it improves per student** | **Every student generates a verified fact or a correction.** Report No. 4 is better than No. 1 because forty families have found the errors in it |
| **Evidence it works** | Report No. 1 already corrected four of our own figures. **The mechanism has been tested on us before it is tested on the market** |

**Second discovery channel, entirely passive: Google Business Profile.** One-time verification, then it
produces high-intent enquiries forever with no human input. **It is the only asset that works while
everyone is counselling.**

**Deleted from discovery: everything that needs someone to post.** Daily social, LinkedIn presence,
YouTube. **They fail the test — they stop the day the team gets busy, which is the day they'd matter.**

## 2. TRUST ENGINE

**The correction, published on a cadence.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | Every report carries a **"What changed since the last report"** section naming what we got wrong |
| **Trigger** | The next report's publication date. **Not a decision** |
| **Why it builds trust faster than accuracy does** | Anyone can be right once. **Only a long record can show you correct yourself** — and the record cannot be created retroactively |
| **Improves per student** | Students surface errors we cannot find alone |

**Paired rule from the Constitution: a correction is always published with the primary source that
resolved it.** *"We were wrong; here is the university's own page"* reads as rigour. *"We were wrong
again"* reads as amateurism.

## 3. REFERRAL ENGINE

**A stage gate, not a campaign.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | **Automatic, on Visa Approved** — the moment of maximum gratitude, and it never comes twice |
| **The ask** | *"If anyone from your school asks you about this, send them our report."* **An introduction, not a sale** |
| **Why it survives Rahul's absence** | The CRM fires the task. **A counsellor executes a step; nobody has to remember a campaign** |
| **Never** | **No referral fee, ever.** A paid referral is an advertisement; an unpaid one is a recommendation |
| **Improves per student** | **The referring node becomes WARM — 3× conversion on their next forward** |

**Build note: the trigger does not exist yet.** `advanceStudentJourney` fires a task at Enrolled +14
days but nothing at Visa Approved. **One line, when there is a first student. Not before.**

## 4. REVIEW ENGINE

**Already built. It fires without anyone deciding to.**

`advanceStudentJourney` creates *"Ask for success story consent"* **14 days after Enrolled.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why 14 days after enrolment, not at graduation** | Gratitude decays. **A student two weeks in remembers who answered the phone at midnight; a graduate remembers the degree** |
| **Consent-first** | The task asks for consent before the story. **Constitution principle 11** |
| **Why it needs no founder** | It is a scheduled task on a record. It fires whether or not anyone is thinking about marketing |

## 5. COMMUNITY ENGINE

> ### 🔴 DELETED. Not deferred — deleted.

**A community needs members. RichenQuest has zero students.** A "community" of prospective families
with nobody who has actually gone is a support group for anxiety, and it will be dominated by the
loudest misinformation in the room — **the precise opposite of what this company sells.**

**Trigger to reconsider: 25 students who have arrived.** At that point the alumni are the community and
it builds itself. **Before that, building it would create a liability we then have to moderate.**

## 6. METRICS

**Four numbers. Each answers *"is this working without Rahul?"***

| Metric | Healthy | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **Reports published on schedule** | **1 per quarter, no slippage** | A missed date means the engine depends on a mood |
| **Forwards per report** | **rising per issue** | Measures whether the series is being awaited |
| **WARM nodes** | **rising** | The only number that compounds |
| **Enquiries arriving with no outbound action that week** | **> 0, then rising** | **The direct test.** If this is zero, everything above is theatre |

**The last one is the whole scoreboard.** Every other number can be produced by someone working hard.
**Only that one proves the system works while the team is busy.**

## 7. FIRST 90 DAYS

| Weeks | What | Founder time |
|---|---|---|
| **1–2** | Approve Report No. 1. **Contact 30 trust nodes** (E1a). Start GBP verification | **~4 hours total** |
| 3–4 | Log every objection verbatim. **12 identical refusals → stop and redesign the ask** | 0 |
| 5–8 | Report No. 2 drafted from what the first 30 conversations revealed. Nodes that forwarded get it first | 0 |
| 9–12 | **Report No. 2 published on its date.** Measure forwards per issue against No. 1 | ~1 hour |

**Founder time across 90 days: under six hours.** Everything else is a scheduled task, a published
document, or a node acting on their own.

**And the honest limit:** the engines above create *awareness*. **They cannot create the first thirty
node contacts** — that requires a person who knows people, and for now that person is Rahul. **After
those thirty, the system runs without him. Before them, there is no system.**
