Company introduction

RICHENQUEST PVT LTD is an education-mobility company based in Patna, Bihar, India. We work with students and families in India and Nepal who are planning English-taught degrees in Europe, and we support them across the full journey — course selection, application, documentation, and the practical questions that decide whether a plan actually works.

About RichenQuest

We are an early-stage company, and we would rather say so than imply a scale we do not have. What we can describe precisely is how we operate:

  • Every course option we present is verified before a family sees it. Tuition, living cost, application deadline, a source URL and a verification date — all five, or the option is not shown at all.
  • We do not use admission, visa or scholarship probability scores. Nobody can predict those honestly, so we do not put a number on them.
  • Bad news travels on the same schedule as good news. Where a student's timing has been lost for an intake, we say so on the day we know.
We make no claim to any existing university partnership. This proposal is an opening approach, and any collaboration described here begins only once both parties agree it.

Student learning benefits

  1. Verified-source discipline. Interns learn to treat an unverified figure as unusable — a habit that transfers to any research or analytical career.
  2. Real casework, supervised. Work on live student cases, never on invented exercises, with a named manager accountable for the output.
  3. Cross-border exposure. European admissions systems, documentation standards and institutional correspondence.
  4. Written communication under a claims standard. Interns learn to write material that survives review for accuracy.
  5. A certificate that means something, because it is issued only on recorded completion.

Mentorship

Each intern is assigned a named reporting manager for the whole internship. The mentorship structure is:

  • A written scope of work and objectives at the start, agreed before day one.
  • A scheduled weekly review with the reporting manager.
  • Mid-point feedback in writing, so there is time to act on it.
  • A final assessment that forms the basis of the certificate.

Industry exposure

Interns work inside the same operational systems the company runs on — CRM casework, verified opportunity data, and the service standards that govern student communication. They see how a student file moves from first enquiry to decision, including the cases that do not succeed and why.

Evaluation process

  1. Objectives set and agreed in writing at the start of the internship.
  2. Weekly supervision against those objectives by the reporting manager.
  3. Mid-point written feedback, with time remaining to act on it.
  4. Final assessment covering work completed, quality and reliability.
  5. Outcome recorded in the HR system as Completed or otherwise — honestly.

Certificate process

A Certificate of Internship is issued only where the internship status is recorded as Completed. The certificate names the role, department, dates, duration, the work completed and the assessment, and carries a unique certificate number your institution can quote back to us for verification. Where an internship ends early or objectives are not met, we will say so rather than certify otherwise — a certificate that is issued regardless of outcome is worth nothing to your students.
