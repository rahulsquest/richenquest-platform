# Internship Offer Letter — paste into Zoho People → Letter Templates

> ⚠️ **Stipend is NOT assumed.** The template supports paid and unpaid through one conditional
> block. Delete the branch that does not apply, or drive it from a Zoho People condition on
> `${Internship Type}`.

---

**Ref:** RQ/HR/INT/${Year}/${Sequence}  **Date:** ${Offer Date}

**${Intern.First Name} ${Intern.Last Name}**

**Subject: Internship Offer — ${Internship Role}**

Dear ${Intern.First Name},

We are pleased to offer you an internship with RICHENQUEST PVT LTD.

| | |
|---|---|
| Role | ${Internship Role} |
| Department | ${Department} |
| Reporting Manager | ${Reporting Manager} |
| Start Date | ${Internship Start Date} |
| End Date | ${Internship End Date} |
| Duration | ${Internship Duration} |
| Work Mode | ${Work Mode} |
| Location | ${Location} |
| Working Hours | ${Working Hours} |
| Internship Type | ${Internship Type} |

**▸ IF `Internship Type = Paid` — include:**
> **Stipend.** You will receive a stipend of **₹${Stipend Amount} per ${Stipend Frequency}**,
> payable on ${Stipend Payment Day}. This is a stipend for a learning engagement and is not
> salary or wages.

**▸ IF `Internship Type = Unpaid` — include:**
> **This is an unpaid internship.** No stipend, salary or other monetary compensation is
> payable. Reasonable pre-approved expenses will be reimbursed.

**Nature of the engagement.** This is a **learning engagement**, not employment. It does not
create an employer–employee relationship and carries no expectation of employment on
completion.

**What you will work on.** ${Learning Objectives}

**Confidentiality.** You will treat all student, family, institutional and company information
as confidential, during and after the internship. You will handle student personal data only
as your role requires.

**Intellectual property.** Work product created in the course of and for the purposes of this
internship belongs to RICHENQUEST PVT LTD. `[REQUIRES COMPANY/LEGAL CONFIRMATION]`

**Code of conduct.** You will follow company policies and act professionally toward students,
families, colleagues and institutions.

**Certificate.** A Certificate of Internship will be issued **on successful completion of the
full agreed duration** and satisfactory performance. **It is not issued for an internship that
ends early or is withdrawn.**

**Withdrawal.** Either party may end the internship with ${Internship Notice Period} written
notice.

**Acceptance**

Signature ____________  Name ____________  Date ____________

For **RICHENQUEST PVT LTD** — ${Authorised Signatory Name}, ${Authorised Signatory Designation}
