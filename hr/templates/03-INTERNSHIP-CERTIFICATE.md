### Wording rules — not stylistic

- **"has completed"** is used, not *"successfully completed"*, unless a performance field on the
  record supports the stronger claim. A certificate asserting success that nobody assessed is a
  false statement, and it follows the intern into future employment.
- The certificate states **what was done and for how long**. It makes no claim about
  performance, aptitude or suitability for employment.
- If the record shows `Withdrawn`, `Terminated` or `Extended`, **the template must not run.**
