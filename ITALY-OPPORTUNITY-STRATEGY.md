## What DSU actually is — verified today at source

From the **ER.GO 2026/2027 bando** (`er-go.it`, `regione.emilia-romagna.it`), verified
2026-08-23:

- **ISEE threshold €25,000** for scholarship and accommodation access.
- International students must submit foreign income and asset documentation for 2025,
  **"complete, translated and legalized"**, by the application deadline — **"under penalty of
  being ineligible for benefits."**
- Students whose family is **resident abroad** are treated under the *fuori sede* provisions —
  the highest band, the one that includes housing.
- Applications go through `er-go.it` or an **affiliated CAF**.
- An **ISEE certification with an INPS protocol number** is mandatory.

**The bottleneck is not the university. It is the ISEE Parificato and the legalised document
pack**, and it has a hard deadline with total forfeiture attached. That is precisely the
failure a consultancy prevents, and it is why the repo prices the Italy package at ₹1.2L
against ₹60k for February.

## Selection criteria — corrected

Because the grant is nationally floored, ranking by generosity is close to noise. Rank by:

| # | Criterion | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Regional DSU body's accommodation capacity** | *Fuori sede* students need housing; a grant without a bed is a partial grant. This is what genuinely varies. |
| 2 | **Regional body's international-student process maturity** | ER.GO and Piemonte publish English guidance and CAF routes; some regions do not |
| 3 | **English-taught programmes at the university** | Without this nothing else matters |
| 4 | **Public status** | DSU applies to public-system institutions |
| 5 | **Verification availability** | Public Italian universities publish tuition bands and deadlines |

Note the unit is the **region first, university second** — DSU is administered regionally
(ER.GO, ERDIS, ADISU, EDISU), not by universities.
