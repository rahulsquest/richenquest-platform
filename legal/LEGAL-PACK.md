# 1 · Privacy Policy

**{{ENTITY}} — Privacy Policy** · Version 1.0 · Effective {{DATE}}

## 1.1 Who we are
{{ENTITY}}, {{ENTITY_TYPE}}, registered at {{ADDRESS}}. We are the **Data Fiduciary** for
personal data you give us, as that term is used in India's Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). You are the **Data Principal**.

## 1.2 What we collect
- **Identity and contact:** name, date of birth, address, phone, WhatsApp number, email.
- **Academic:** qualifications, marksheets, transcripts, degree certificates, backlogs,
  study gaps, English test results or Medium of Instruction letters.
- **Financial:** budget range, family income band, funding source, bank statements and
  loan sanction letters where a visa application requires proof of funds.
- **Travel and immigration:** passport details, visa history including any prior refusal.
- **Family:** parent or guardian name and contact details.
- **Technical:** IP address and browser type when you use our website.

We do not collect data about your religion, caste, political opinions or health unless a
specific university or visa application requires it, in which case we will ask you for it
explicitly and tell you why.

## 1.3 Why we process it, and on what basis
We process your data on the basis of the **consent you give** when you submit an
application, for these purposes only:

| Purpose | What it involves |
|---|---|
| Assessing eligibility | Matching your profile against course and country requirements |
| Advising you | Preparing shortlists, cost estimates and timelines |
| Applying on your instruction | Submitting applications to universities you approve |
| Supporting your visa | Preparing your file; you sign and submit it yourself |
| Arrival support | Accommodation and residence-permit registration |
| Legal and tax records | Retaining invoices and agreements as law requires |

## 1.4 Who we share it with
- **Universities and their admissions partners** — only those you have approved by name.
- **Visa authorities and their appointed service providers** — only where your own
  application requires it.
- **Our technology providers** — Zoho Corporation (CRM and accounting).

**We do not sell your data.** We do not pass it to other consultancies, to lenders, to
insurers, or to anyone for marketing purposes.

## 1.5 Cross-border transfer
Applying to a foreign university necessarily means sending your data outside India. By
approving a university, you approve the transfer of your application to it and to the
relevant visa authority. We transfer nothing beyond what that application requires.

## 1.6 How long we keep it
See the Data Retention Policy (§7). In short: while you are an active client, plus
{{RETENTION_YEARS}} years for financial and legal records, after which we delete.

## 1.7 Your rights under the DPDP Act
You may at any time:
- **Access** a summary of what we hold about you and who we have shared it with.
- **Correct** anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
- **Erase** your data, where we are not required by law to keep it.
- **Withdraw consent** — as easily as you gave it. Message {{WA_NUMBER}} or email
  {{EMAIL}} with the word WITHDRAW.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
- **Complain** to us first, and to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not
  resolve it.

**Withdrawing consent stops future processing. It does not undo what a university or a
visa authority has already received**, because we cannot recall a submitted application.
We will tell you plainly what stage you are at before you withdraw.

## 1.8 Children
If you are under 18, a parent or lawful guardian must give consent before you submit
anything to us. We do not knowingly process a child's data without it, and we do not
track children or serve them advertising.

## 1.9 Security
Your data is held in Zoho CRM under access controls. Access is limited to staff working
on your file. Documents are stored against your record and not on personal devices. If a
breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection
Board as the DPDP Act requires.

## 1.10 Grievance officer
**{{GRIEVANCE_NAME}}**, {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}}, {{ADDRESS}}.
We will acknowledge within 3 working days and resolve within 30 days.

**2.3 What we do not guarantee.** We do not and cannot guarantee: admission to any
university; the award of any scholarship; the grant of any visa; any particular
processing time; employment, salary or permanent residence after study. **Admission is
decided by the university. A visa is decided by a government. Neither decision is ours,
and no fee paid to us changes either.**

**2.4 Accuracy of what you tell us.** Our advice is only as good as your information. You
confirm that everything you give us is true and that your documents are genuine.
**Submitting a forged or altered document is a criminal offence and grounds for us to
terminate immediately with no refund.** We will also be obliged to inform the university
or authority concerned.

**2.5 Cost figures.** Every cost we give you carries its source and the date we checked
it. Tuition, living costs, visa fees and exchange rates change without notice and are set
by third parties. Our figures are estimates prepared in good faith, not quotations we
control.

**2.6 Your own signature.** You sign your own application forms, declarations and visa
paperwork. We will not sign on your behalf and will refuse any request to do so.

**2.7 Third-party costs.** University application fees, tuition deposits, blocked-account
deposits, visa fees, insurance, courier and translation charges are paid by you, directly
to those parties. They are not ours, and refunds of them are governed by whoever charged
them.

**2.8 Communication.** We will communicate by WhatsApp, phone and email. You agree that
written updates sent to the number and email you gave us are validly delivered.

**2.9 Liability.** Our total liability to you is limited to the fees you have actually
paid us. We are not liable for visa refusals, admission rejections, university changes of
policy, appointment availability, or delays at any government office.

**2.10 Termination.** Either of us may end the engagement in writing. Refunds are governed
by §4.

**2.11 Governing law.** These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at
{{JURISDICTION}} have exclusive jurisdiction.

# 4 · Refund Policy

**4.1 The principle.** You pay for work, in stages. You are refunded for work not yet done.
We do not keep money for a stage we did not reach.

**4.2 Refund table**

| When you cancel | Refund of our fee |
|---|---|
| Within **7 days** of signing, before any application is submitted | **100%** |
| After counselling and shortlist, before any application submitted | **75%** |
| After applications submitted, before any offer received | **50%** |
| After an offer is received | **25%** |
| After a visa file has been prepared and submitted | **No refund of our fee** |

**4.3 What is never refundable by us.** University application fees, tuition deposits,
blocked-account deposits, visa fees, insurance premiums, courier, translation and
attestation charges. These are paid by you to third parties and are governed by them.

**4.4 Visa refusal.** A visa refusal is **not** a service failure and does not by itself
create a refund entitlement, because the decision is a government's. However:

> **If we advised you to pursue an intake that our own records show was already
> unreachable when we advised it, we will refund 100% of our fee.** Our system computes
> and records the last safe filing date for every case. If that date had passed when we
> told you to proceed, that is our error and we will pay for it.

This clause is deliberate. It is the one refund promise we can make honestly, and it is
enforceable against us from our own audit timeline.

**4.5 If we fail to deliver.** If we miss a deadline through our own inaction and you lose
an intake as a result, you may cancel and receive a full refund of our fee.

**4.6 How to claim.** Email {{EMAIL}} with your case number. We acknowledge within 3
working days, decide within 15, and pay within 15 of deciding, to the account the payment
came from.

# 6 · Cookie Policy

The RichenQuest portal sets **no cookies and no third-party trackers**. There is no
analytics script, no advertising pixel and no social embed.

The application wizard stores your unfinished answers in your own browser's local storage
so you can close the page and come back. That data never leaves your device until you
press Submit, and clearing your browser data removes it.

*If a founder later adds analytics or advertising, this policy must be rewritten and
a consent banner added before that code ships.*

# 7 · Data Retention Policy

| Category | Retained | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiries that never became clients | 24 months from last contact | Deleted |
| Active client records | Duration of engagement | Moves to the row below |
| Completed client records | {{RETENTION_YEARS}} years from final invoice | Deleted |
| Invoices, receipts, agreements | {{RETENTION_YEARS}} years (income-tax requirement) | Deleted |
| Uploaded documents (passport, financial) | 12 months after case closes | **Deleted** |
| Visa refusal grounds, de-identified | Indefinite | Retained — see below |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn, plus 3 years | Deleted |
| Audit timeline (`Case_Events`) | 7 years | Retained |

**Financial and identity documents are deleted at 12 months, not 7 years.** We keep the
invoice, not the bank statement. Holding a family's financial papers longer than the work
requires is a risk to them with no benefit to us.

**Refusal grounds are kept indefinitely with the student's name removed.** They are the
most valuable operational data this company will ever hold and every future student
benefits from them. The record retains country, mission, refusal ground and month — never
the person.

**Erasure requests** are honoured within 30 days, except where a record must be kept for
tax law. We will tell you which specific records we are keeping and why.
