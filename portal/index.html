Questions families actually ask

What do you charge?

Our fee is quoted in writing before you commit, alongside the full cost of the country and course you choose. We will not give you a number on this page that we have not yet agreed with you in person, because a number without its scope is how families get misled.

Can you guarantee admission or a visa?

No, and neither can anybody else. Admission is decided by the university and a visa is decided by a government. Any consultancy that guarantees either is either lying or doing something you do not want to be part of.

Do I need IELTS?

Not always. Many European universities accept a Medium of Instruction letter from your college, and some run their own English interview. Which applies to you depends on the specific university, and we will tell you which before you spend money on a test.

I do not have a passport yet. Is that a problem?

It is the most common reason a family misses an intake. A new Indian passport takes weeks. Apply this week regardless of how far away your intake feels — everything else can happen in parallel, but nothing can happen without it.

Will you handle my visa interview or sign my forms?

No. We prepare you and we check your file, but you sign your own declarations and you attend your own appointment. It is your application, legally and practically, and anyone offering to sign on your behalf is putting you at risk.

What happens to my documents and my data?

See the privacy notice directly below. Short version: we store it in our CRM, we share it only with universities and authorities you approve, and you can ask us to delete it.
