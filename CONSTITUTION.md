# The RichenQuest Constitution

**Written to survive a change of CEO.** Four documents: the Constitution, the Trust Ledger, the
Student Promise, the Institutional Operating Principles.

**Version 1.0 · 17 August 2026**

---

# 0. Two of my own assumptions, attacked first

### 🔴 The moat is narrower than I claimed

**I have argued repeatedly that True Cost is uncopyable because commission-funded agencies cannot
afford to publish it. That is true of IDP, AECC and KC Overseas. It is false of LeapScholar and
Leverage Edu.**

**They monetise loans and forex, not enrolments. Publishing honest costs would cost them nothing** —
arguably it would help them, since a family that trusts your numbers trusts your loan.

> **So the moat is not the incentive. It is the time.** Anyone can publish a cost table tomorrow.
> **Nobody can retroactively have been wrong in public and fixed it for five years.** The correction
> history is the asset, and it cannot be bought, hired or accelerated.

**This changes the strategy's urgency, not its direction: the sooner the record starts, the wider the
gap becomes.**

### 🔴 "Students first, trust second, revenue third" is not operable

**A company with no revenue serves no students.** A strict ranking would justify running out of money
while feeling virtuous.

> **The honest structure is not a ranking: students are the purpose, trust is the constraint, revenue
> is the fuel.** No decision may breach the constraint. Within it, maximise the fuel. **The purpose is
> what both exist for.**

# I. THE CONSTITUTION

**Twelve principles. None may be violated by any employee, including the founder.**

**On truth**

1. **Every factual claim is VERIFIED, UNKNOWN, or DISPUTED. Never blank, never implied.**
2. **We say "I don't know" before we guess.** That answer has never cost this company a student.
3. **When two official sources disagree, we publish the disagreement.** We do not choose the convenient one.
4. **We publish our own corrections, in public, naming what we got wrong.** *(Five to date, including a ₹9 lakh error in our own cost model.)*
5. **We never state a number without a source and the date it was checked.**

**On money**

6. **We are paid by families, never by universities in a way that alters our advice.** Any commission received is disclosed to the family before the recommendation.
7. **We charge for deliverables we control, never for outcomes we do not.**
8. **We do not charge for our own mistakes.**
9. **We never recommend a university because it pays more. Ranking is by fit, and the reasoning is shown.**

**On people**

10. **We tell a family when Europe does not work for them**, and we do it before they spend anything.
11. **The student is an adult.** We do not report on a student to anyone — including their parents — about their own conduct.
12. **We never promise admission, a scholarship, a visa, a job, or a faster appointment.** Nobody can deliver these, and everyone who promises them is lying.

**Amendment:** a principle may be amended only in writing, with the reason recorded, and the amendment
published. **Principles 4, 6, 11 and 12 may not be amended at all** — they are the ones a struggling
company would be most tempted to bend.

# II. THE TRUST LEDGER

**Every initiative does one of three things. Score it before starting.**

| | Definition | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **+ ADDS trust** | Costs us something now, earns belief | **Do it even at a loss, within reason** |
| **− SPENDS trust** | Earns now, costs belief later | **Requires the founder's written approval** |
| **= CONVERTS trust** | Charges for something we have earned the right to sell | **The healthy default** |

### Scored — current initiatives

| Initiative | Score | Note |
|---|:-:|---|
| **Free Clarity Report**, including "Europe doesn't work for you" | **+++** | The single largest deposit available |
| **Publishing our own corrections** | **+++** | Compounding, uncopyable |
| **Refusing to quote unverified living costs** | **++** | Reads as competence, not evasion — §IV.3 |
| **Waiving the fee stage on our own error** | **++** | |
| **DSU stage converts to credit if unawarded** | **++** | |
| Permit & Compliance Care | **=** | Genuine annual need, honestly priced |
| Scholarship Care | **=** | Protects ₹13 lakh; earns its fee |
| Staged pricing | **=** | Risk shifted to us |
| Seminars | **=** | 30 minutes of use before any selling |

| Paid advertising | **=** | Neutral, and neutral is not free — deleted at this stage |

**Anything scoring neutral and consuming resource is deleted.** Neutral processes are how
institutions become companies.

# III. THE STUDENT PROMISE

**One page. Every employee knows it. Every promise is measurable.**

> **To every student and every family:**
>
> **1.** We will tell you what your education will **actually cost** — tuition, living, fees, and the money you lose if the answer is no — **before you spend anything.** *Measurable: a written report, every figure sourced and dated, at no charge.*
>
> **2.** We will tell you **what we do not know**, by name. *Measurable: every report has a "what we have not verified" section.*
>
> **3.** We will tell you if **Europe does not work for your budget**, even though it costs us the sale. *Measurable: we track how many families we turn away.*
>
> **4.** We will never promise you **admission, a scholarship, or a visa** — and we will tell you that anyone who does is lying. *Measurable: zero such claims in any asset; enforced automatically.*
>
> **5.** You will pay most of our fee **only when there is an offer in your hand.** *Measurable: staged payment, published.*
>
> **6.** If we make a mistake that costs you, **we will not charge you for it**, and we will tell you it was ours. *Measurable: fee stage waived, in writing.*
>
> **7.** We will plan backwards from the **last safe date to file your visa**, not forwards from today. *Measurable: every case carries that date from day one.*
>
> **8.** We will not disappear when you land. **We will keep your permit valid and your scholarship intact for as long as you study.** *Measurable: renewal reminders before every annual deadline.*
>
> **9.** We will never tell your parents about your **attendance, your marks or your spending.** We report on our own work, not on you. *Measurable: no student-conduct data leaves this company, ever.*
>
> **10.** When we are wrong, **we will publish the correction.** *Measurable: corrections published, counted monthly.*

# IV. INSTITUTIONAL OPERATING PRINCIPLES

## 1. What actually makes institutions trusted

**Not branding. Incentive design.**

| Institution | The operational fact |
|---|---|
| **Mayo Clinic** | **Physicians are salaried, not paid per procedure** — no financial reason to over-treat |
| **ISRO** | **Failures are announced publicly**, with the cause |
| **Amul** | **The supplier owns the company** |
| **Tata** | Trusts own the firm — **the time horizon is generational** |

**Which RichenQuest can legitimately build:**

- **The Mayo property — already true.** We are paid by the family, not per enrolment. **This is the single most important structural fact about the company and it must never be traded away**, however attractive a commission becomes.
- **The ISRO property — buildable today.** Publish the failures.
- Amul's and Tata's ownership structures: **not available. We will not pretend otherwise.**

## 2. 🔴 The Family Line, deleted and rebuilt

**I designed a parent membership, then added a consent requirement. Attacking it properly, the consent
does not hold.**

**A 21-year-old whose parents are paying ₹30 lakh cannot freely refuse when asked to consent to a
parental report. That is not consent. It is compliance.**

> **So the service is rebuilt: RichenQuest reports to parents on RichenQuest's work — permit status,
> deadlines met, applications submitted, money we have taken and what it bought. It never reports on
> the student's conduct: not attendance, not marks, not spending.**
>
> **We report on ourselves, not on the student.** The coercion problem disappears because there is
> nothing personal to consent to — and the parent gets what they actually wanted, which is to know
> somebody competent is watching.

**Principle 11 exists so no future CEO can reverse this when a parent offers to pay more.**

## 3. The honesty risk, named

**A company that says "I don't know" often can read as incompetent rather than honest.** This is a
real risk in a market where confidence sells.

> **The differentiator is not uncertainty. It is precision about where certainty ends.**

**Wrong:** *"We're not sure what it costs."*
**Right:** *"Tuition is USD 7,500 a year — that is from their own page, checked on 16 August. Living
costs I have not verified, and I will not give you a number I cannot stand behind. I will have it in
a week."*

**The second is more confident than a competitor's guess, not less.** Every employee must be trained
on this distinction, because getting it wrong makes the Constitution sound like weakness.

## 4. Failure manual

**How an institution responds — the difference is that an institution answers before it is asked.**

| Failure | Response |
|---|---|
| **Visa refused** | Grounds recorded verbatim, same day. Curable or substantive, stated honestly. **If the ground is our documentation error: fee stage waived, error admitted in writing, refusal added to the log** |
| Admission rejected | Reasons sought from the university. Alternatives within 72 hours |
| Scholarship not awarded | **DSU stage converts to a credit** for re-application |
| **Deadline missed by us** | **Told the same day, before they discover it.** Founder calls the parent. Full remedy offered |
| **Wrong advice given** | Corrected in writing to the family within 24 hours; **corrected publicly if it was published** |
| Staff mistake | The system is fixed, not the person blamed. **A mistake that can recur is a process defect** |
| **Negative review** | **Replied to publicly, by name, without defensiveness.** If they are right, say so |
| Public criticism | **If accurate, agree publicly and publish the fix.** If inaccurate, correct once with evidence and stop |
| **A published number proves wrong** | **Correction published within 48 hours**, naming what was wrong and why |

**The rule under all of them: the family hears it from us first.**

## 5. Knowledge philosophy

**Knowledge exists to improve student outcomes, not to attract traffic.**

| We do | We do not |
|---|---|
| Verify what a family would otherwise be lied to about | Publish for search volume |
| Write an article when a question is asked **twice** | Write to a content calendar |
| Delete knowledge that goes stale | Keep pages for SEO after they stop being true |
| **Publish what we have not verified** | Imply completeness |

**Test before publishing anything: *would we still write this if nobody read it but one family?***
**If no, it is marketing wearing knowledge's clothes.**

## 6. Revenue philosophy

**Trust is the constraint. Revenue is the fuel. Students are the purpose.**

**Three questions before any revenue line:**

1. Does it solve a problem that genuinely recurs?
2. Would we recommend it if it earned us nothing?
3. Could a family reasonably feel sold to rather than served?

**Any "no", "no", or "yes" — delete it.**

**Deleted under this test:** SIM and travel partnerships *(no real problem)* · career services *(we have no employer network)* · paid slot services *(fraud)* · any success fee on a scholarship *(outcome we do not control)*.
