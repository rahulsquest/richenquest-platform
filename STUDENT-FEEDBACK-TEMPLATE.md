# STUDENT-FEEDBACK-TEMPLATE.md

Sent after the student has read their report. **Under two minutes.** Every question is here
because an answer would change what we build; nothing is here to make the product look good.

---

## Four ratings — 1 to 5

| # | Question | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Relevance** — how well did the options match what you actually want? | matching |
| 2 | **Clarity** — how clearly did we explain *why* each option was suggested? | explainability |
| 3 | **Trust** — how much did you believe the numbers we gave you? | verification thesis |
| 4 | **Usefulness** — how useful was the plan of what to do next? | roadmap |

*1 = not at all · 3 = somewhat · 5 = completely*

## Three open questions

**Q5. Was anything recommended that you already knew was wrong for you?**
> The single most valuable question here. A confident wrong recommendation is worse than no
> recommendation, and only the student can spot it.

**Q6. Did you learn anything you did not already know?**
> If the answer is consistently no, RichenQuest is a search engine with better manners and the
> thesis is wrong.

**Q7. Would you have found this yourself, given a week?** *(0–10)*
> Measures whether this is worth paying for.

## One question for Cohort A only

**Q8. We told you we do not have verified Italian options yet. How did that make you feel
about us?**
> *(more confident / less confident / no difference — and why)*
>
> This tests the riskiest assumption in the product: **that families reward honesty about
> gaps.** If admitting a gap loses trust, verification-first is a costly principle rather
> than a moat, and we need to know early.

## Improvement categories — how answers are routed

| Signal | Category | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Low relevance (Q1 ≤ 2) | matching or data | check the data first; **3 students before touching logic** |
| Low clarity (Q2 ≤ 3) | `UX_ERROR` | wording — never the score |
| Low trust (Q3 ≤ 3) | provenance | are sources visible enough? |
| Low usefulness (Q4 ≤ 3) | roadmap | are actions specific and dated? |
| Q5 flags a wrong option | **investigate immediately** | data or logic |
| Q6 mostly "no" | **thesis risk** | founder |

## Rules
Collected **after** the counsellor call, so the student is rating the product and not the
person. Never incentivised — a paid rating is not data. And a low score is a finding, not a
complaint to be managed.
