# ITALY-PATHWAY-MODEL.md — 2026-08-23

Italy is not a university list. It is a **regional-process product**, and the university is
the least decisive variable in it.

## ⭐ The finding that changes the product

**EDISU Piemonte states that a non-EU student does NOT need to obtain an ISEE Parificato
before applying** — EDISU calculates the parificato value itself, with CAAF offices under
agreement, from consular documentation.

> *"Students do not have to get an ISEE Parificato before applying: the ISEE parificato
> values will be calculated by EDISU Piemonte directly, with the assistance of the CAAF
> offices under agreement."* — `edisu.piemonte.it`

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** Found on the official domain, but the source PDFs are font-encoded
and the requirements page 404s, so I could not confirm it on the page itself.
**Verification email sent to `edisu@edisu-piemonte.it` on 2026-08-23 16:32.**

**If confirmed, it removes the single hardest step in the Italy product.** The repo has

## The pathway, in the order a student actually experiences it

```
1  Admission to a public university          (university's own deadline)
2  Regional DSU application                  (regional bando deadline - EARLIER, and absolute)
3  Consular income + family documents, legalised by the Italian Prefettura
4  ISEE parificato   → obtained via CAF, OR calculated by the body (Piemonte)
5  Scholarship + accommodation outcome
6  Visa, using the DSU outcome as part of proof of means
7  Arrival → residence permit within 8 DAYS
8  Year-two credit compliance                (protects €14-16k, annually)
```

**Steps 2-4 are where students lose the money, not step 1.** ER.GO's bando states foreign
income documentation must be *"complete, translated and legalized"* by the deadline **"under
penalty of being ineligible for benefits."** Total forfeiture, on a paperwork deadline.

## Region comparison — the real unit of selection

The grant is **nationally floored**, so ranking regions by generosity is close to noise
(recorded finding). What actually varies:

| Region / body | ISEE Parificato in advance? | International process | Accommodation pressure | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Piemonte / EDISU** | **Provisionally NO** — body calculates it | English guidance, CAAF agreements, ticketing | Turin: large capacity | **1st — pending reply** |
| **Emilia-Romagna / ER.GO** | Yes — ISEE with INPS protocol required | Most mature; ISEE threshold €25,000 verified | Bologna: high pressure | 2nd |
| **Marche / ERDIS** | Not verified | Founder already researching | Lower pressure, smaller system | 3rd |
| **Sicily / ERSU** | Not verified | Messina and Palermo in founder's browsing | Low pressure, lowest living cost | 4th |

**Accommodation capacity is the tie-breaker**, not grant size: a *fuori sede* student with a
grant and no bed has a partial grant. Bologna's desirability is a liability here.

## Student readiness requirements — Italy-specific

| # | Requirement | Lead time | Fails if |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family income documents, **legalised by Prefettura** | **Months** | Late → total ineligibility |
| 2 | Family composition certificate, legalised | Months | Same |
| 3 | Full-year income detail, not partial months | — | ER.GO: partial-month certificates **"not acceptable"** |
| 4 | Correct income year | — | Extra-EU: the year the bando names |
| 5 | Admission before/alongside the bando | Varies | Bando closes first |
| 6 | Residence permit within **8 days** of arrival | 8 days | Legal right to remain |
| 7 | Year-two credit compliance | Annual | Loses €14-16k |

**Requirement 1 is the product.** It has a hard deadline, total forfeiture, and no second
chance — exactly what a family cannot self-manage from Bihar.

## Why this is a September product
Regional bandi close in late summer / early autumn. A February 2027 starter cannot apply.
Recorded ☠️ **DEAD** in the ledger and unchanged by this research.

## What must NOT enter the CRM
No Italian university until tuition, living cost, **the regional bando deadline** (not the
