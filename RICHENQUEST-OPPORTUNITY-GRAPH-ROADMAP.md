## 1 · Pilot strategy
Two products, two intakes, one engine.

**February 2027** — Central European **public** universities. Modest margin, ₹20–30L
families, but it is the cohort that is running now and the only one that produces evidence
this quarter.

**September 2027** — **Italy with DSU**, ₹0–3.5 lakh to the family. The only option in reach
of the stated ₹10–25L segment, and the one the ₹1.2L package is priced for.

The pilot's job is not to prove the engine. It is to find where the engine is wrong about a
real person, and February is simply when real people are available.

## 8 · Why these match RichenQuest customers

The target family has **₹10–25 lakh** and is in Bihar or Nepal.

- **Debrecen at ₹30 lakh is outside that.** It is a real, verified option and it is not the
  product for this customer. Positioning it as the default would be selling the wrong thing.
- **Central European public universities** land inside the band and — proven across eight
  institutions — publish the data the verification gate requires.
- **Italy with DSU at ₹0–3.5 lakh is the only option genuinely inside the segment**, and its
  difficulty is precisely the reason a family cannot self-serve it: legalised consular
  documents, a regional deadline that forfeits everything, an 8-day permit window, annual
  credit compliance. **That difficulty is the business.**
- **German private universities fail on both axes** — above segment and unverifiable.

The graph is small on purpose. Two verified options a family can act on are worth more than
twenty they cannot check, and the verification gate is what makes that claim true rather than
a slogan.
