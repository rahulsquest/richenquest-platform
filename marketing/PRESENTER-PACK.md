## 1. Read this before anything else — the five sentences that end the company

**Delegating a seminar is the single largest brand risk RichenQuest will take this quarter.** One
presenter improvising one promise to one hall of parents is more damaging than never running the
seminar at all — because in Bihar and Jharkhand a consultancy's reputation travels by word of mouth,
and a broken promise travels faster than a kept one.

**You may never say any of these. Not as an aside, not to be encouraging, not because someone pushed.**

| ❌ Never | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| *"You'll definitely get admission / a scholarship / a visa."* | *"You meet these requirements and not those. Nobody can promise the outcome."* |
| *"Your chances are good."* | *"We don't give percentages. Anyone who does is making it up."* |
| *"Total cost is around ₹X."* | *"Tuition is X. Living costs for Hungary we have not verified and will not guess at."* |
| *"We're partnered with that university."* | *"We've verified their requirements against their own site. We've asked about a formal partnership."* |
| *"We can get you a faster visa appointment."* | *"Nobody legitimately can. We start early so you don't need one."* |

**If you are unsure whether something is true, say: *"I don't know — I'll confirm and send it to you
in writing."*** That answer has never lost RichenQuest a student. A wrong answer has no upside at all.

> **Saying "I don't know" in front of 100 people is the brand.** Slide 13 of the deck says so
> explicitly. You are not weakening the pitch — you are delivering it.

## 2. What this role is

**You deliver the 45-minute seminar and make the first call.** You do **not**:

- decide fees or discounts → **Rahul**
- promise a university a partnership → **Rahul**
- give legal, visa-outcome or migration advice → **nobody at RichenQuest does this**
- counsel a family to a final university choice → **that is the counselling session, booked separately**

**Your job is to be useful for 30 minutes, honest for 15, and collect six fields.**

## 3. Three-day training path

| Day | Do this | Ready when |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Read `SEMINAR-KIT.md` end to end. Read the Debrecen and Vistula knowledge articles in the CRM. **Memorise §1 above** | You can recite the five never-says from memory |
| **2** | Deliver the full 45 minutes to two colleagues. **They interrupt with hostile questions** — *"how much total?", "guarantee milega?", "IDP is free, why pay you?"* | You got through it without a promise |
| **3** | Shadow one first call. Then make one, with Rahul listening | You asked *"is that the whole budget or per year?"* without prompting |

**Nobody presents alone before day 3.** A seminar is 100 people; the cost of an unready presenter is
not one bad talk, it is a hall full of families with a wrong number in their heads.

## 4. Pre-flight — the morning of every seminar

- [ ] **Open each university's CRM record and check `Review_Date` has not passed.** If it has, **that university does not go in the talk.** Figures change; a stale record is how a presenter says something untrue in good faith
- [ ] Confirm **Debrecen: 1 November 2026** is still the stated deadline — verified 16 Aug 2026 from `edu.unideb.hu`
- [ ] Print **capture slips × attendance + 20%**
- [ ] Print the deck. **Do not rely on a projector you have not tested**
- [ ] Carry a pen box. *(Half your slips are lost to people who had no pen.)*
- [ ] Phone charged — you will run `leadToPlan` on the drive back

## 5. Delivering it — what actually matters

**The deck is written to be useful for 30 minutes before it mentions RichenQuest.** Do not shortcut
that. A room in Patna has heard the study-abroad pitch before.

| Moment | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Slide 4, the "we have not verified living costs" line** | **Slow down. Say it twice.** This is the single line that separates RichenQuest from everyone who has spoken in that hall before |
| **Slide 7, Vistula's waiver condition** | **Read the condition out loud.** Leaving it off makes more of the room interested and is the reason a student loses a semester in Warsaw |
| **Slide 9, the Italy DSU slide** | Expect the room to lean in. **Then say the hard part**: for 2027 that means September, not February |
| **Slide 13, "what we will not do"** | **This is the close.** Do not rush it and do not apologise for it |
| Anyone asks about cost | Give the number **and** the non-refundable figure in the same breath |
| **A question you cannot answer** | *"I don't know — I'll confirm and send it in writing."* **Write the question down.** It is the most valuable thing you will take home |

**Hand out slips at minute 38, not at the end.** People leave during Q&A.

## 6. Same day, without exception

- [ ] Every slip entered as a Lead, **`Lead_Source_Detail` = institute name**
- [ ] Run `leadToPlan` on each — you arrive at the first call already knowing the shortlist
- [ ] **Debrief written within 24 hours** (`WAR-ROOM-7-WEEKS.md` §4). Every question verbatim, every parent concern in the words used, which slide lost the room
- [ ] **Any question asked at two seminars → knowledge article that week**

> **A slip in a bag on Monday is a lost lead by Thursday.** This is the step that quietly kills
> campaigns, and it is the easiest one to skip after a long day.

## 7. Daily checklist

**Morning** — leads from yesterday all entered? · calls due today? · any lead older than 48 hours *(that is a failure, fix it first)* · next seminar confirmed?

**Evening** — every call logged? · every new lead has budget, intake, English, phone? · anything you could not answer written down?

## 8. Your KPIs

| Metric | Target | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **Slips ÷ attendance** | **>15%** | Measures the talk, not the room |
| **Leads called within 48h** | **100%** | The only number fully in your control |
| Qualified ÷ leads | >60% | Measures slip quality and call discipline |
| Counselling sessions booked | >40% of qualified | Measures the first call |
| **Questions logged per seminar** | **>5** | **A zero here means you were not listening** |
| Promises made | **0** | **Non-negotiable. One is a failed review** |

**Notice what is not measured: enrolments.** That is not yours yet, and paying you attention on it
would push you toward promises.

## 9. Stop and get Rahul

- Anyone asks about **fees, discounts or refunds** — F1 is not set; **do not improvise a number**
- A university or institute wants a **written agreement**
- A family mentions a **previous visa refusal** — this needs proper handling, not a seminar answer
- Anyone asks about **work visas, PR or migration** — **regulated. We do not advise on it**
- You realise you said something that was not true → **tell Rahul the same day.** A correction sent within 24 hours protects the relationship; one discovered later ends it
