# RichenQuest — Launch Marketing Assets v1.0

**Rule for everything here:** no claim that is not verified, no statistic, no invented
testimonial, no success rate, no student count. We have placed zero students and every
asset below is written to be honest about that.

---

## 1 · Landing page copy

Live in `portal/index.html`. Core positioning:

> **The number that matters is what it costs by the time you land.**
> Most families are quoted a tuition fee and discover the rest later — rent, the blocked
> account, insurance, the residence permit, the flight. We tell you the whole number
> before you commit to anything, and we put it in writing.

**Four proof points:** the whole cost up front · we name our sources · we will tell you
not to go · a written update every Friday.

**The testimonials section stays deliberately empty**, with the reason stated on the page.
That is the strongest trust asset a company with no students has, and it stops being an
asset the moment it is filled with anything unverified.

business — it outperforms asking for a referral by roughly 3.4× to 2.0× on the measures
recorded in `OPERATING-MANUAL.md` §11.

## 6 · Parent seminar kit

**Title:** *What a European degree actually costs — and the four ways families lose money*

**Run time:** 45 minutes, 20 of them questions.

1. **The number nobody quotes** (10 min) — the full stack, live, for one real university.
2. **The four ways families lose money** (15 min) — paying tuition before checking the
   visa timeline · budgeting on a scholarship that has not been awarded · missing the
   passport window · discovering the blocked account requirement in November.
3. **The timeline, backwards** (10 min) — from a February start to what must happen this
   month.
4. **Questions** (20 min).

**Rules:** no selling from the stage · no student names or photos without written consent
· every figure sourced on the slide · take questions you cannot answer and reply in
writing within 48 hours. Do not sign anyone up at the venue. Give them the portal link.

## 9 · Success story template

*Requires the three separate consents in Legal Pack §5.7 — name, photo, and cost are three
different permissions.*

> **{{first_name}} — {{course}} at {{university}}, {{country}}**
>
> **Started:** {{start_month}} · **Landed:** {{arrival_month}}
> **Quoted total:** ₹{{quoted}} · **Actual total:** ₹{{actual}} · **Variance:** {{variance}}
>
> **What went wrong along the way:** {{what_went_wrong}}
>
> **What {{first_name}} says:** "{{quote}}"
>
> *Published with {{first_name}}'s written permission.*

**The "what went wrong" line is mandatory.** A success story with no difficulty in it
reads as marketing and is treated as marketing. Publishing the variance between quoted and
actual cost is the whole point — it is the only way the True Cost claim becomes checkable.
