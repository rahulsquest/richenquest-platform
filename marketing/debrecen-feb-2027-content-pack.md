## 7. Visa guide (Hungary) — ☐ approved

Draw only from knowledge article *"Ireland, Poland, Hungary and Malta — appointment routes"*.

- D-visa via **VFS Global** (Delhi / Mumbai jurisdictions), then convert to a residence permit **within 30 days of arrival**.
- Processing **~2 weeks reported** — a range, **never a promised date**.
- Slots are scarce at peak; the November–December run-up to a February intake is the peak.
- **We do not use slot bots.** They breach the site's rules and put *your* application at risk.
- **Last safe filing date is computed per student** from the course start date.

## 8. LinkedIn article — ☐ approved

**Title:** *We publish what we haven't verified. Here's why.*

Angle: most study-abroad marketing states living costs, salary outcomes and visa success rates with
total confidence and no source. RichenQuest's university records carry `Verified_On`, `Source_URL`,
`Confidence` and `Review_Date`, and the matching engine **refuses to recommend a university whose
data is unverified** — so a counsellor sometimes has to say *"we don't have a verified option for
you yet."*

**Worked example: Debrecen.** We publish the tuition, the fees, the English rule and the gap rule —
and we publish that we have **not** verified living costs, work rights or employability, so we won't
discuss them. Audience: partner universities and the industry, not students.
