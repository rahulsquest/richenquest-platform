The gap between the brochure and the bill

We researched the University of Debrecen in Hungary carefully. It is a good university with a real advantage for Indian students, and we recommend it to the right family.

Its tuition for an English-taught Master's is USD 7,500–8,000 a year — about ₹13 lakh across two years. That is the number most families are shown.

The University of Debrecen's own cost-of-living page says students in Debrecen spend about USD 800 per month. Over two years that is roughly ₹16.7 lakh — more than the tuition.

Four things nobody mentions

  1. The application fee is not the application cost

    Debrecen charges USD 150 to apply. There is also an entrance examination fee reported at USD 350. That is roughly ₹43,000 spent before anyone has looked at your file — and it is not refundable if the answer is no.

    Not verified  We have asked the university to confirm the exam fee applies to Master's applicants. Until they reply, treat it as unconfirmed.

  2. Where you live can change the total by ₹5–12 lakh

    A university dormitory in Debrecen is reported at HUF 40,000 a month. Private rental, on the university's own accommodation page, is HUF 120,000–140,000. Over two years that difference is larger than most families' entire savings for the degree.

    We have asked whether dormitory places are guaranteed for self-funded international students. The university's own page does not say they are. Until we know, we plan on the higher number.

  3. Germany's €11,904 “blocked account” is not a cost

    It is your own money. It sits in a German account and is released back to you at about €992 a month to live on. Germany is a liquidity barrier — you must be able to produce the sum — not a price. Anyone describing it as a fee has not understood it.

  4. Poland's student work rules changed, and much of the advice online has not caught up

    Since 1 December 2025, a non-EU student in Poland can work without a separate work permit only if their university is on an approved Ministry of the Interior list — and most Polish universities are not.

    If someone tells you your child can simply work part-time in Poland, ask them which list the university is on.

The one that is genuinely worth knowing

Italy's regional DSU grant is open to non-EU students, and it is means-tested on family income rather than marks alone.

The Emilia-Romagna authority's own rules state that an international student whose family does not live in Italy is awarded at the “fuori sede” rate — the highest band — regardless of where the student actually lives. For 2026/27 the national minimum for that band is €7,171.11 a year, and DSU winners also have tuition waived and can receive accommodation and meal support.

With DSU, an Italian public-university Master's can cost a fraction of the Hungarian total. Without it, Italy is still roughly ₹13–25 lakh.

Three warnings, because this is the number families most want to believe

  • It is not guaranteed. It is applied for, against income thresholds and available funding.
  • It arrives after enrolment. You must be able to fund the course and the visa before it.
  • Applications close in early autumn — typically July to September, before most families have even chosen a university. For a September 2027 start, that means preparing now.

Anyone who tells you they can guarantee you this scholarship is lying to you.

The number nobody can give you

How much must you show for a Hungarian student visa?

Hungary's immigration authority requires you to prove you can cover subsistence, accommodation, return travel and healthcare — and publishes no figure at all.

We found secondary sources confidently stating HUF 200,000 a month and others stating HUF 98,000 — a difference of more than double.

They disagree because there is no published number to agree on. If a consultant quotes you a precise amount, ask where it comes from

How to check us

Every figure above is from a primary source and dated. Check them.

  • edu.unideb.hu · unideb.hu — tuition, deadlines, cost of living, accommodation
  • oif.gov.hu — Hungarian residence permit fees and requirements
  • er-go.it · edisu.piemonte.it — DSU rules and amounts

If you find we are wrong, tell us. We will correct it publicly and credit you. We have already corrected our own Debrecen estimate four times as we moved closer to primary sources — and each time the real number went up.

That is the whole reason this report exists.
