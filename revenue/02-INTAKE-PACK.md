## Form 7 · Risk Assessment

```
[ ] No passport / expired          → CRITICAL PATH, weeks to fix
[ ] Intake < 4 months away          → timeline may already be lost
[ ] Budget short                    → will surface at deposit, not now
[ ] Loan not sanctioned             → proof of funds will fail
[ ] Prior visa refusal              → changes everything; get the letter
[ ] Study gap > 2 yrs unexplained
[ ] Backlogs outstanding
[ ] Parent not aligned              → the most common late collapse
[ ] English evidence missing
[ ] Documents not in student's name / spelling mismatch

Top risk named to the family today: _______________________________
Their response: ___________________________________________________
```

> A counselling session that named no risk was a sales call. Record at least one.
